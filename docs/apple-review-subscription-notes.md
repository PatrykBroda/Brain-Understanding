# App Review Notes — FRAME+ Subscriptions

## Notes field

FRAME+ subscription details are shown before purchase on the in-app FRAME+ screen.
Each plan displays its App Store product title, localized price, subscription
length, and confirms that all listed FRAME+ services are provided for that
subscription period. The same screen includes functional Privacy Policy and
Terms of Use (EULA) links below the auto-renewal disclosure.

To review:

1. Sign in.
2. Open Profile.
3. Tap the FRAME+ upgrade card.
4. Review the plan title, localized price, billing period, included services,
   renewal terms, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use (EULA) before purchasing.

## Screen recording checklist

Record one continuous clip that:

1. Opens Profile and taps the FRAME+ upgrade card.
2. Pauses on the included FRAME+ services.
3. Shows each plan's title, localized price, and subscription period.
4. Shows the auto-renewal and cancellation disclosure.
5. Taps Privacy Policy and shows the loaded policy page.
6. Returns to FRAME+, taps Terms of Use (EULA), and shows the loaded terms page.
7. Returns to the paywall and shows Restore Purchases.

## App Store Connect metadata checklist

- Put `https://ajsajjajds.com/api/privacy` in the Privacy Policy field.
- Put `https://ajsajjajds.com/api/terms` in the App Description or EULA field.
- Confirm both URLs are public, use HTTPS, and load without authentication.
