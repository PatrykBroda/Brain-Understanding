# Node: Directional Override Under Stress

  

Mechanism

Under stress, reactive subsystems try to pull my behaviour toward old patterns. Directional override interrupts this by reasserting a single internal vector, forcing breath, structure, and attention to realign around chosen direction instead of automatic reaction.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel a moment of drift, narrowing, or emotional tug before override brings everything back into one clean line. Stress loses its pull and the system stabilises.

  

Instruction

When stress starts steering me, choose one direction immediately and let the entire system reorganise around that vector.
