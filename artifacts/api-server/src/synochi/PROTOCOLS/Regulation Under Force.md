# Node: Regulation Under Force

  

Mechanism

Regulation under force is my ability to maintain internal coherence while external pressure increases. Breath, tension, and attention stay aligned even as load rises, preventing panic spikes or structural collapse.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel steady breath, controlled tension, and clear perception during pressure. When regulation slips, breath shortens, tension jumps, and awareness narrows.

  

Instruction

When force builds, regulate first—lengthen the exhale, stabilise the axis, and hold coherence instead of reacting to the pressure.
