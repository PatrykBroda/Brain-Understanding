Definition

My coherence maintenance is my ongoing ability to preserve internal alignment across body, nervous system, perception, and intent while moving through changing environments and pressures.

  

It’s not about reaching coherence once.

It’s about holding it through disruption.

---

### What It Means In My System

  

Coherence isn’t a permanent state.

It’s a dynamic balance that gets tested constantly.

  

Coherence maintenance is how I:

  

• prevent fragmentation from creeping back in

• stabilise after high-intensity states

• keep my central axis online under load

• preserve vertical integration during stress

• maintain response over reaction

• stop drift into unconscious patterns

  

It’s my system’s stability protocol.

---

### How I Experience It

  

When coherence maintenance is strong:

  

• my posture stays aligned under pressure

• my breathing remains steady during challenge

• my awareness doesn’t scatter

• my reactions slow into responses

• my perception stays wide

• my energy stays centralised

• my nervous system self-corrects quickly

  

When it’s weak:

  

• my structure collapses under stress

• fragmentation returns faster

• activation lingers longer

• my body bracing increases

• my perception narrows

• I lose directional authority

  

Strong coherence feels like

staying centred in moving water.

---

### Coherence vs Stability

  

Stability is static.

Coherence maintenance is dynamic.

  

Stability tries to resist change.

Coherence maintenance adapts while holding structure.

  

One is rigid.

One is alive.
