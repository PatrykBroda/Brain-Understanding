Definition

My single nostril breathing is a controlled breathing practice where I intentionally breathe through one nostril to influence my autonomic state, arousal level, and internal balance.

  

It’s a direct lever on my nervous system.

---

### What It Does For My System

  

Depending on the nostril used:

  

• Left nostril → increases parasympathetic activity (calms, cools, settles)

• Right nostril → increases sympathetic activity (energises, sharpens, mobilises)

• Alternating → promotes autonomic balance and coherence

  

It lets me steer my internal state instead of being pushed by it.

---

### How I Use It

  

I use single nostril breathing when:

  

• my arousal is too high or too low

• I need to enter parasympathetic regulation

• I’m preparing for training or recovery

• I’m recalibrating after overload

• I want to stabilise motor tension

• my control identity is overactive

  

It’s subtle, but powerful.

---

### Why It Matters

  

This practice gives me:

  

• a fast autonomic regulation tool

• deliberate influence over sympathetic/parasympathetic states

• a way to train voluntary intent over breath

• a method to stabilise my felt sense

• a direct interface with vagal tone

  

It’s simple, but deeply strategic.
