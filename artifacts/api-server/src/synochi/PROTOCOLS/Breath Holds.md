## Definition

  

Breath Holds are deliberate suspensions of respiration used to modulate arousal, regulate the CNS, and redistribute internal energy.

  

They are not breathwork.

They are arousal valves.

---

## Function

  

Breath Holds operate as:

- A pressure regulator for [[Arousal]]
    
- A signal disruptor during [[Hijacking]]
    
- A stabiliser during [[Nervous System Recalibration]]
    
- A conduction amplifier for [[Conduction of Power]]
    

  

They create internal stillness under load.

---

## Role in Your System

  

Breath Holds sit at the intersection of:

- [[Sensory Anchoring]]
    
- [[Attention Control]]
    
- [[Central Reorientation]]
    
- [[Directional Authority]]
    

  

They allow you to pause motion without losing direction.

---

## Types

  

• Passive Hold – after exhale, parasympathetic dominant

• Active Hold – after inhale, sympathetic compression

• Dynamic Hold – during movement or transitions

  

Each type interacts differently with [[Arousal]] and [[Momentum]].

---

## Risks / Misuse

  

Breath holds fail or fragment when:

- Done with tension instead of containment
    
- Used to escape sensation
    
- Combined with overstimulation
    
- Forced outside capacity
    

  

Result: dizziness, dissociation, energy leakage.
