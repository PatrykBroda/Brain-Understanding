Signal

Aggression becomes most dangerous when it leaks unconsciously.

  

Rule

Contained aggression increases capability; expressed aggression reduces optionality.

  

Protocol

- Acknowledge aggressive energy internally
    
- Do not discharge it socially or verbally
    
- Channel it only into chosen domains (training, execution)
    
- Return to neutrality immediately after use
    

  

Stop Condition

If aggression bleeds into tone, posture, or conversation, containment has failed.
