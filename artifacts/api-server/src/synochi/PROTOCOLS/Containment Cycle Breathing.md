
I use this breath to train how I generate, hold, and release internal pressure without fragmentation.

  

Pattern:

10s inhale → 15s hold → slow relaxed exhale

  

This isn’t just calming. It’s a controlled pressure test.

  

When I inhale for 10 seconds, I’m loading the system.

When I hold for 15 seconds, I’m practicing containment under stress.

When I release slowly, I’m training controlled discharge without collapse or overshoot.

  

This directly trains the same skill I need in mount and side control:

holding pressure without leaking, rushing, or fragmenting.

---

### What it trains in my system

  

• My tolerance to internal pressure without panicking

• My ability to stay relaxed while arousal rises

• My solar plexus pressure control

• My fragmentation resistance under load

• My CNS ability to stay coherent during stress

  

It reinforces this principle:

Pressure doesn’t break me — it clarifies my structure.

---

### How I apply it

  

I run 5–10 cycles before training, sometimes between rounds.

I also use it when I feel overstimulated, scattered, or when I need to re-centre authority in my body.

  

It’s not for relaxation.

  

It’s for containment, control, and strategic calm.
