# Node: Cortisol Curve Awareness

  

Mechanism

My cortisol rhythm shapes energy, focus, and emotional tone throughout the day. When I track the curve — rises, dips, and spikes — I understand how my system naturally regulates stress and performance. Awareness gives me leverage; ignorance creates misinterpretation.

  

Behavioural Expression

I notice predictable windows: sharpness in the morning, dips in mid-day, evening sensitivity, or unexpected spikes during stress or anticipation. These shifts affect breath depth, tension patterns, and decision capacity.

  

Instruction

When my internal state shifts, reference the cortisol curve first — recognise the current position, reduce misinterpretation, and adjust output or regulation accordingly.
