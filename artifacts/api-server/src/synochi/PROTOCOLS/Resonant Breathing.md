Definition

My resonant breathing is a slow, rhythmic breathing pattern that synchronises my heart rate, nervous system, and body into a coherent, regulated state.

  

It’s my fastest way to stabilise internal rhythm.

---

### What It Does For My System

  

When I breathe at resonance:

  

• my heart rate variability increases

• my autonomic nervous system balances

• my arousal stabilises

• my perception clears

• my stress response softens

• my central axis steadies

  

It creates internal coherence through rhythm.

---

### How I Use It

  

I use resonant breathing when:

  

• my arousal spikes

• I start fragmenting

• I need parasympathetic entry

• my jaw or face tightens

• after BJJ rounds

• before sleep

• during nervous system recalibration

  

I breathe slow and even.

Long exhale.

Steady cadence.

---

### Why It Matters

  

Resonant breathing is:

  

• one of my quickest regulation tools

• a bridge between mind and nervous system

• a stabiliser for threshold dynamics

• an entry point to parasympathetic regulation

• a conductor for somatic coherence

  

It’s simple, but structural.
