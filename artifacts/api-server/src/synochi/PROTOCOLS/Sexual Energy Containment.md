# Node: Sexual Energy Containment

  

Mechanism

Sexual energy is raw physiological charge. When contained, it circulates inward through the system and strengthens focus, presence, and structural integrity. When it leaks outward through fantasy, tension release, or impulsive behaviour, the system loses coherence and pressure.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel heat, pulse, or drive rising in the lower abdomen and chest. When containment is strong, the energy stays compressed and sharpens intention. When containment breaks, I feel scatter, restlessness, or drop in internal pressure.

  

Instruction

When sexual energy rises, contain it — direct the charge upward through the axis, hold structure, and keep the pressure internal instead of dispersing it outward.
