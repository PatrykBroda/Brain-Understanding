# Node: Predictive Delay Compression

  

Mechanism

Predictive delay compression reduces the time between recognising a pattern and acting on it. By tightening the gap between perception and execution, the system shifts from reactive timing to anticipatory control without rushing or hesitating.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel cleaner timing — my body moves closer to when my mind recognises the opportunity. When compression fails, there’s noticeable lag, second-guessing, or delayed initiation.

  

Instruction

When timing feels slow, close the gap by committing earlier — trust the recognised pattern and let action follow immediately behind perception.
