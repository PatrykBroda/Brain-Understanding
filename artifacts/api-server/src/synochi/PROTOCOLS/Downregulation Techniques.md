## 1. 8 – 2 – 6 Breathing

  

This is my stabilisation breath.

  

Inhale 8 seconds

Hold 2 seconds

Exhale 6 seconds

  

It slows my system without dulling it.

It trains CO₂ tolerance, stabilises vagal tone, and supports parasympathetic dominance without collapse.

  

I use this when:

- arousal is elevated but manageable
    
- I want to stay alert but grounded
    
- I’m transitioning out of intensity
    

---

## 2. Box Breathing (4x4x4x4)

  

This is my structural reset breath.

  

Inhale → Hold → Exhale → Hold

Each for 4 seconds.

  

It reorganises my internal rhythm when my attention or arousal feels scattered.

  

I use this when:

- I feel fragmented
    
- my mind is racing
    
- my perception feels chaotic
    

---

## 3. Left Nostril Breathing

  

This is my parasympathetic override.

  

I close my right nostril and breathe only through my left.

  

It quiets threat scanning, reduces adrenaline tone, and stabilises vagal activity.

  

I use this when:

- I feel overstimulated
    
- emotional or sexual energy is too active
    
- perception is too sharp
    

---

## 4. Breath Holds (Retention Control)

  

Breath holds train my system to stay calm inside pressure.

  

This isn’t restriction.

It’s controlled exposure.

  

I use short, calm holds — never forced.

  

After inhale:

Hold for 3–6 seconds

Then slow controlled exhale.

  

After exhale:

Hold for 2–4 seconds

Then slow controlled inhale.

  

This helps me:

- expand my stress tolerance
    
- stabilise arousal under load
    
- develop internal containment under pressure
    

  

It strengthens my ability to stay coherent in stillness, not just movement.

  

I use this when:

- I want to train internal resilience
    
- I’m strengthening control under arousal
    
- I’m reinforcing central stability
    

---

## 5. Inverted Breathing

  

This is my body-based nervous system recalibration.

  

Instead of the chest rising,

I let the lower abdomen gently expand first,

then the ribs,

then the chest.

  

On exhale, everything softens back down.

  

This shifts the breathing centre downward and stabilises my thoracic and vagal system.

  

It improves:

- pelvic and diaphragmatic connection
    
- nervous system grounding
    
- energetic containment
    
- central alignment
    

  

I use this when:

- I want to bring energy down from my chest/head
    
- I feel upper-body tension or agitation
    
- I’m grounding after high activation
