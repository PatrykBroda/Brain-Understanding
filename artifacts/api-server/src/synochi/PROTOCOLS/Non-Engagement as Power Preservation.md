Signal

Not every stimulus deserves a response; restraint preserves power.

  

Rule

Engagement is a cost. Withholding response maintains internal authority and prevents energy leakage.

  

Protocol

- Notice invitations to react or participate
    
- Decline silently when value is low
    
- Maintain neutral behaviour and tone
    
- Redirect attention to chosen priorities
    

  

Stop Condition

If I feel compelled to respond just to resolve discomfort or uncertainty, engagement is already draining power.
