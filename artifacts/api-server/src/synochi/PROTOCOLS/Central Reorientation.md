
Central Reorientation

Behavioural Expression

I feel my posture restack, breath deepen, and attention snap back into a single channel. Noise drops and the system becomes deliberate again.

  

Instruction

When I sense hijacking, reorient immediately by returning to the axis and reclaiming directional authority.

It’s not suppression.

It’s directional override.

  

I do this by:

• relocating attention to my central axis

• stabilising my breath + arousal

• re-establishing vertical conduction of power

• overriding reaction with controlled orientation

  

The result is authority returning to centre instead of being siphoned into noise.

  

This is my internal power recapture mechanism.
