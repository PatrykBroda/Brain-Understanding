## Definition

  

Momentum is the inertial force of directed energy once a vector has been chosen.

It is what carries action forward after intention has already been set.

  

Not motivation.

Not will.

Not emotion.

Directional force in motion.

---

## Core Principle

  

> Momentum is not created by speed.

> It is created by alignment + continuity.

  

When direction, energy, and structure are aligned, motion sustains itself.

---

## Functional Role

  

Momentum sits between:

- [[Power of the Agent of Transformation]]
    
- and [[Directional Authority]]
    

  

It is the transmission phase.

  

Not the spark.

Not the controller.

The carry force.

---

## What It Is

- The continuation of movement after choice
    
- The energetic inertia of aligned direction
    
- The force that reduces friction through consistency
    

---

## What It Is Not

- Not hype
    
- Not adrenaline
    
- Not emotional drive
    
- Not speed
    
- Not compulsion
    

  

Momentum is clean.

Controllable.

Directional.

---

## System Relationships

  

Momentum emerges from:

## Somatic Expression

  

Momentum is felt in the body as:

- Forward pull through the [[Central Axis]]
    
- Elastic tension in fascia
    
- Reduction of internal friction
    
- Transitional flow between positions
    

  

Often experienced as:

Not force — but inevitability.

---

## Directional Types

  

• Upward Momentum – expansion, elevation

• Forward Momentum – execution, life motion

• Inward Momentum – consolidation, integration

• Downward Momentum – grounding, compression

  

Each must be regulated by [[Arousal]] and stabilised through [[Sensory Anchoring]].

---

## Failure Modes

  

Momentum collapses when:

- Direction is unclear
    
- [[Central Axis]] is unstable
    
- Energy leaks via [[Hijacking]]
    
- The system fragments instead of conducts
    

  

This leads to:

False acceleration.

Burnout.

Scattered force.
