# Node: Distributed Circulation of Power

  

Mechanism

Power becomes stable when it circulates through multiple structural routes instead of bottlenecking in one area. Distributed circulation uses fascia, spirals, and the axis to spread load, preventing collapse and producing smoother force output.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel balanced tension across the body — hips, ribs, spine, and shoulders sharing the load. When circulation collapses, tension spikes in one spot, movements become jerky, and force output feels uneven.

  

Instruction

When power feels stuck or concentrated, widen the pathways: soften gripping, re-engage spirals, and allow force to travel through the whole structure.
