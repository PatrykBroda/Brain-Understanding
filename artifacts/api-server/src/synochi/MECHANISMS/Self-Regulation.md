## [[Self-Regulation]]

  

Type: [[Core Control Node]]

  

Definition:

The capacity of a system or human to modulate internal state, response timing, and action selection in order to preserve [[Coherence]] and [[Authorship]] under load.

  

Core Statement:

Regulation precedes intelligence; unregulated intelligence amplifies error.

  

Mechanism:

Self-regulation governs the gap between [[Stimulus]] and [[Response]], allowing [[Refusal]], [[Delay]], or [[Narrowing]] before execution.

  

Primary Functions:

- Maintain [[Baseline Coherence]]
    
- Prevent [[Runaway Reactivity]]
    
- Enable [[Judgment]] under pressure
    
- Preserve [[Refusal Capacity]]
    

  

Regulatory Levers:

- [[Breath]] / [[Physiological Modulation]]
    
- [[Attention Allocation]]
    
- [[Arousal Control]]
    
- [[Pacing]]
    
- [[Constraint Holding]]
    

  

Failure Indicators:

- [[Impulsivity]]
    
- [[False Urgency]]
    
- [[Cognitive Flooding]]
    
- [[State Hijack]]
    
- [[Thought Parasitism]]
    

  

System-Level Application (Synochi):

- Introduce [[Delay]] when signal degrades
    
- Reduce output under high [[Arousal]]
    
- Shift from [[Assistance]] to [[Resistance]]
    
- Enforce [[Silence]] when needed
    

  

Invariant:

No decision is valid outside a regulated state.

  

Failure Modes if Absent:

- [[Reactive Obedience]]
    
- [[Context Collapse]]
    
- [[Authority Loss]]
    
- [[Error Amplification]]
