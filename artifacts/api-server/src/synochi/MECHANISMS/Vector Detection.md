Vector isn’t detected through thoughts.

It’s detected through coherence patterns across body, attention, and behaviour.

  

Here’s how it shows up.

---

### Signs I’m 

### On Vector

  

I don’t feel hyped.

I feel quietly directional.

  

• My decisions feel simple, not heavy

• My attention doesn’t split easily

• My body feels structurally stacked (posture is natural, not forced)

• My generation of energy feels smooth, not frantic

• I don’t narrate myself – I just move

• I don’t need motivational input

• There’s minimal internal debate

• I feel like a clean current, not a storm

  

The key sign:

I’m not fighting direction — I’m moving with it.

---

### Signs I’m 

### Drifting Off Vector

  

Drift doesn’t feel dramatic.

It feels like subtle loss of coherence.

  

• I start over-explaining myself internally

• I need more stimulation to feel “alive”

• My posture loses vertical integrity

• My breathing gets shallow or chest-heavy

• I feel busy instead of directed

• I start compulsively checking / seeking / switching

• I feel reactive to things that wouldn’t normally move me

• I feel like I’m orbiting, not advancing

  

The key sign:

I’m doing things, but none of them feel like they’re moving forward.

---

### Signs I’m 

### Off-Vector

  

This is deeper misalignment.

  

• I feel scattered but overactive

• My arousal spikes without direction

• My body feels tense or collapsed

• My decisions feel urgent but unclear

• I feel performative instead of embodied

• I start justifying things a lot

• My sense of time warps (either rushed or dull)

  

The key sign:

My system is moving, but without a trajectory.

---

## Real-Time Vector Check Method

  

Here’s a fast internal diagnostic:

  

Ask yourself:

1. Does my movement feel like advance or avoidance?
    
2. Does this feel like trajectory or distraction?
    
3. Is my energy directed or just intense?
    

  

If it’s direction…

You’re on vector.

  

If it’s just intensity…

You’re drifting.

---
