### **MECHANISM**

**Uncertainty-to-Measurement Conversion**  
A regulation process in which speculation, prediction, or narrative generation is replaced by objective measurement. By converting uncertainty into observable data, cognitive load decreases and decisions become grounded in reality rather than imagined outcomes.
