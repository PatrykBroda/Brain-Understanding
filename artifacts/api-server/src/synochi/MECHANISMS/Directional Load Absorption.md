# Node: Directional Load Absorption

  

Mechanism

Directional load absorption lets my system take incoming force along chosen vectors rather than absorbing it blindly. By aligning structure, fascia, and breath with the path of force, I convert impact into controlled movement instead of collapse or bracing.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel incoming pressure travel cleanly through spirals or the axis instead of hitting a dead end. When this fails, load hits me flat, causing gripping, flinching, or sudden rigidity.

  

Instruction

When force comes in, meet it with a chosen direction—guide the load through structure instead of resisting it head-on.
