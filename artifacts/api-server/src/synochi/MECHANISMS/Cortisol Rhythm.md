My cortisol rhythm is the daily stress–energy wave that determines how mobilised or receptive my system is across the day.

  

When it’s healthy, it supports performance without burning me out.

  

My ideal pattern:

- Cortisol peaks in the morning → gives me drive, alertness, and mobilisation
    
- It tapers through the day → lets me stay productive without overdrive
    
- It’s low at night → allows melatonin to rise, sleep to deepen, and recovery to happen
    

  

If this curve is distorted:

- I feel wired at night
    
- flat or foggy in the morning
    
- overstimulated without purpose
    
- or drained even after rest
    

  

For my work, cortisol isn’t the enemy.

It’s a mobilisation tool that needs a clean waveform.

  

A stable rhythm supports:

- arousal regulation
    
- training output
    
- nervous system recalibration
    
- sleep integration
    
- testosterone balance
    
- cognitive clarity
    

  

I don’t want low cortisol.

I want well-shaped cortisol.
