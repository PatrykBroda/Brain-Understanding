## Sub-Mechanics

My breath isn’t just oxygen.
It’s the control panel for my central nervous system.

**How I relate to it:**

My breathing is a **direct input** to:
	•	My heart rate
	•	My stress response
	•	My emotional state
	•	My attention
	•	My recovery

It’s the one system I can voluntarily access that talks straight to my autonomic nervous system.

**How I use it as a tool:**

**1. To downregulate (calm my system)**
When I feel wired, anxious, or overstimulated:
	•	Inhale through my nose for 4
	•	Hold for 2
	•	Exhale slowly through my nose for 6–8
	•	Repeat for 3–5 minutes

Longer exhales tell my body: *I’m safe.*

**2. To sharpen focus**
Before training, writing, or decision-making:
	•	Inhale for 4
	•	Hold for 4
	•	Exhale for 4
	•	Hold for 4
	•	4–6 rounds

This balances my system without sedating it.

**3. To release stored tension**
When I feel emotion stuck in my body:
	•	Deep nasal inhale into my diaphragm
	•	Slow exhale through slightly parted lips
	•	Let the belly soften on exhale
	•	No forcing, just dropping

I treat breath like **draining static from a machine**.

**My core understanding:**

My nervous system cannot lie to my breathing.
And my breathing cannot lie to my nervous system.

So instead of fighting my mind,
I speak directly to the system beneath it.
