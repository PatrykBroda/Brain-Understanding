# Breath-Tension Synchronisation

## Mechanism
Breath-Tension Synchronisation is the coordination of breathing and tension as external pressure rises. In Synochi's existing framework, it is part of maintaining signal coherence under stress and regulation under force.

## Behavioural Expression
Coordinated breath and controlled tension accompany clear perception under pressure. When coordination breaks down, breath shortens, tension spikes and attention narrows.

## Application
Use the existing regulation guidance when pressure increases: lengthen the exhale, stabilise the axis and bring breath, tension and attention back into alignment.

This describes coordination within the existing framework. It does not prescribe one breathing cadence for every action.
