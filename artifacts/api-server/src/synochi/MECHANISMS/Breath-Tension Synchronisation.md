# Node: Emotional Impulse Decoupling

  

Mechanism

Emotional impulses arise faster than deliberate intention. Decoupling separates the initial surge from the behavioural output, giving the system space to choose direction instead of acting from the first internal spike.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel a quick rise of heat, urgency, or pressure, but instead of moving with it, the body pauses while the mind stays clear. When decoupling fails, impulse and action fuse, creating reactive behaviour.

  

Instruction

When an emotional impulse hits, hold still for one breath and let the surge pass through without attaching behaviour to it.
