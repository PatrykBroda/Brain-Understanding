## Permeability 

  

Permeability, in my system, is not about being “open” or “closed” socially or emotionally.

  

It is about how information, energy, presence and influence are allowed to move through me, and where they are routed once they’re inside.

  

I don’t see my nervous system as a wall.

I see it as a membrane with intelligent gates.

  

This membrane doesn’t just block or protect.

It routes.

  

It determines:

- what enters my field of awareness
    
- how strongly it enters
    
- how long it stays active
    
- and which internal systems it interacts with
    

  

When my permeability is distorted:

- I either let too much in and become flooded
    
- or I block too much and become rigid / isolated
    

  

Neither is strength.

  

Strength is selective permeability.

---

### Permeability vs Defence

  

When I’m grounded and regulated, my permeability increases without collapse.

I’m not pushing things out, but I’m not absorbing them blindly either.

  

When I’m in an ego-defensive or overstimulated state, permeability decreases.

Not as power.

But as protection.

  

This contraction isn’t intelligence.

It’s survival circuitry narrowing the channel.

  

So permeability is not a personality trait.

It’s a dynamic state variable of my nervous system.

---

### Permeability as Routing, Not Filtering

  

Most people think of perception as a filter — something that blocks.

  

I see mine more as a routing system:

- Signals aren’t just “blocked”
    
- They are redirected
    
- Downregulated
    
- Reweighted
    
- Or passed into different subsystems
    

  

It’s not about resisting influence.

It’s about deciding where influence goes.

  

This is where it moves beyond psychology and into architecture.

  

My nervous system doesn’t just suppress input.

It redirects energy and information flow through structural pathways.

---

### Why Identity Changes Permeability

  

When my identity is rigid or threatened:

- My system clamps down
    
- Permeability drops
    
- Input becomes noise or threat
    

  

When my identity is relaxed and non-defensive:

- Permeability increases
    
- Signals move deeper into the system
    
- But without hijacking it
    

  

So permeability isn’t about vulnerability.

It’s about how stable my internal structure is while remaining open.

---

### Why This Matters

  

Permeability is the control point.

  

Before:

- direction
    
- action
    
- decision
    
- emotion
    
- or reaction
    

  

Everything passes through permeability first.

  

If I master this, I don’t need to control everything downstream —

I control what’s allowed upstream and how it gets routed.
