

Core Breakdown

  

Catecholamines = dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline

They are my mobilisation chemistry.

They move me from potential → action → adaptation → recovery.

---

### 1. Dopamine — Anticipation & Direction

  

Dopamine is my direction-setting chemical.

  

It governs:

- Motivation
    
- Goal orientation
    
- Anticipation
    
- Reward prediction
    
- Learning through reinforcement
    

  

Cycle role:

  

> It creates forward momentum before action even begins.

  

If dopamine is balanced:

- I feel driven but not compulsive.
    
- Action feels chosen, not hijacked.
    

  

If dysregulated:

- I chase stimulation instead of direction.
    
- I get stuck in loops (scrolling, craving, restlessness).

### 2. Noradrenaline — Readiness & Precision

  

Noradrenaline = my environmental readiness chemical

  

It governs:

- Alertness
    
- Focus
    
- Sensory sharpening
    
- Threat detection
    
- Task engagement
    

  

Cycle role:

  

> It tunes me to the environment and sharpens my ability to respond.

  

Balanced:

- Clear attention
    
- Fast processing
    
- Calm vigilance
    

  

Dysregulated:

- Anxiety
    
- Hypervigilance
    
- Over-reactivity
    
- Tunnel vision

### 3. Adrenaline — Mobilisation & Action

  

Adrenaline = my kinetic output chemical

  

It governs:

- Physical intensity
    
- Strength
    
- Speed
    
- Emergency response
    
- Survival mobilization
    

  

Cycle role:

  

> It converts readiness into physical action.

  

Balanced:

- Power without loss of control.
    
- Execution without panic.
    

  

Dysregulated:

- Shaky energy
    
- Crash after action
    
- Emotional volatility
    
- Burnout cycles

### 4. Clearance & Reuptake — The Reset Phase

  

This is underrated and crucial for your work.

  

After catecholamines peak, the body must:

- Reuptake
    
- Break them down (COMT, MAO enzymes)
    
- Reset receptor sensitivity
    

  

If this doesn’t happen properly:

- I stay overstimulated.
    
- My baseline rises.
    
- My system forgets what calm feels like.
    

  

This ties directly into your:

- Aftershock phase
    
- CNS recalibration
    
- Adaptation consolidation
