
Type: Operator Node (Execution Trigger)

Status: Canonical

Function: Authorises immediate action the moment inevitability collapses hesitation.

---

### Definition

  

This is the binary execution trigger that fires when the system transitions from preparation to action.

  

It is not a decision.

It is a state change.

  

Before the click: waiting is correct.

After the click: delay is error.

---

### Core Doctrine

- Hold indefinitely until the click.
    
- Act immediately on the click.
    
- Never negotiate with the click.
    
- Never manufacture the click.
    

  

This operator protects against both premature force and missed windows.

---

### Why This Operator Exists

  

Most execution failures come from:

- acting before inevitability,
    
- hesitating after inevitability,
    
- confusing emotion with readiness,
    
- confusing readiness with urgency.
    

  

This operator enforces clean temporal authority:

- patience before,
    
- decisiveness after.
    

---

### Click Characteristics

  

The click has consistent features:

- internal debate stops instantly
    
- bodily initiation precedes thought
    
- breath arrests or drops naturally
    
- timing feels late, not early
    
- action feels smaller than expected
    
- no desire to explain or justify
    

  

If explanation is still present, the click has not occurred.

---

### Signal Source

  

The click is generated when:

- constraints have collapsed options,
    
- cybernetic feedback is stable,
    
- insula signal is unambiguous,
    
- identity pressure is absent,
    
- noise has been eliminated.

### Binary Nature

  

The operator is non-gradient:

- not 70% sure
    
- not “almost”
    
- not emotionally charged
    
- not confidence-based
    

  

Either:

- no click → hold, or
    
- click → move immediately
    

  

Anything in between is narrative contamination.
