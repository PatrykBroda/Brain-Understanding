**Boredom** — A signal that the current environment is no longer providing sufficient novelty, challenge, meaning, or adaptation pressure to maintain engagement.

More compressed:

**Boredom** — The nervous system’s detection of diminishing returns from the current stimulus.

**Function:**

Pushes attention toward exploration, variation, learning, or increased challenge.

**Prediction:**

When boredom is ignored, people often seek stimulation rather than progression, leading to distraction, novelty chasing, or self-created problems.
