# Node: Expansion–Contraction Balance

  

Mechanism

My system constantly moves between expansion (widening, opening, increasing capacity) and contraction (narrowing, containing, focusing). Balance keeps the nervous system stable. Too much expansion creates overwhelm; too much contraction creates rigidity.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel widening breath, openness in the ribs, and broad awareness during expansion. I feel tighter focus, denser tension, and inward pressure during contraction. Imbalance shows up as overstimulation or shutdown.

  

Instruction

When I feel too wide, contract slightly; when I feel too tight, expand. Maintain the balance point where the system is both open and controlled.
