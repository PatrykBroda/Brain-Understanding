# Node: Emotional Charge Containment

  

Mechanism

Emotional charge is just accelerated energy in the system. Containment redirects that energy inward instead of letting it leak outward through reactions, impulses, or scattered tension. When contained, charge becomes usable power rather than destabilising noise.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel heat, pressure, or intensity rising in the chest, throat, or face. When containment is strong, the energy stays inside the system and sharpens focus. When containment breaks, it spills into impulsive behaviour, over-talking, or physical jitter.

  

Instruction

When emotional charge rises, seal the system — soften the throat, breathe low, and redirect the energy inward toward the sternum to stabilise it.
