## Solar Plexus

  

I treat the solar plexus as a functional valve, not a chakra or symbolic centre.

  

It’s where mechanical pressure, breath, and nervous system state intersect.

  

It governs how force moves through me — or breaks me.

---

### 1. What it is (Function, not mysticism)

  

The solar plexus sits at the junction of:

  

• diaphragm

• rib cage mechanics

• vagal nerve influence

• enteric (gut) nervous system

• core pressure system

  

It is where:

breathing, pressure, arousal, and threat perception converge.

  

This isn’t metaphorical.

It’s a real physiological intersection.

---

### 2. How it works in my system

  

When my solar plexus is open and regulated:

  

• my breath is low and controlled

• pressure distributes through my structure

• my arousal stays clean and centred

• I remain grounded under stress

• external force doesn’t fragment me

  

When it is collapsed or over-tense:

  

• my breath becomes shallow

• my chest lifts and leaks power

• my CNS moves toward fight/flight

• my perception narrows

• my containment weakens

  

So this point decides whether pressure:

stabilises me or destabilises me.

---

### 3. Solar Plexus in Jiu-Jitsu

  

When I apply pressure through someone’s solar plexus:

  

I’m not just pinning them.

I’m disrupting their internal pressure engine.

  

I collapse:

• their breathing rhythm

• their core stability

• their ability to generate force

• their nervous system composure

  

This is why well-placed pressure from mount or side control

feels suffocating before it even hurts.

  

I’m attacking structure, not flesh.

---

### 4. Solar Plexus in CNS Regulation

  

This region is tightly linked to:

  

• vagus nerve regulation

• autonomic balance (sympathetic / parasympathetic)

• emotional containment

• threat sensitivity

• internal coherence

  

When I regulate this area through breathing and posture:

  

I regulate how stress flows through my entire system.

  

It becomes a control gate for arousal.

---

### 5. How I train this valve

  

I train my solar plexus by:

  

• belly-led breathing instead of chest breathing

• controlled breath pressure under load

• posture that stacks head → ribs → pelvis

• learning to receive pressure without collapsing

• using compression (in BJJ + rest) without panic

  

This strengthens my ability to stay dense, calm and stable under force.

---

### One-line summary

  

My solar plexus is the pressure valve

between physical force and nervous system state.
