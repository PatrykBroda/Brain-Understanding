# Node: Spread vs Focus Dial

  

Mechanism

My attentional field widens or narrows like a dial, not a switch. A wide spread increases perception and reduces threat fixation; a narrow focus sharpens precision and execution. Control of the dial determines whether I operate in awareness mode or action mode.

  

Behavioural Expression

I notice moments where attention spreads too wide and becomes vague, or narrows too sharply and becomes tunnelled. Each state changes breath rhythm, tension distribution, and decision speed.

  

Instruction

When my attention drifts or collapses, adjust the dial—widen to stabilise, narrow to act, and keep it deliberate rather than automatic.
