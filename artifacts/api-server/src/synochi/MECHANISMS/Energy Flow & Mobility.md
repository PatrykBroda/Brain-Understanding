### Energy Flow & Mobility

  

Definition

Energy flow and mobility is how my contained energy moves through my structure without obstruction, distortion, or leakage.

  

Energy is capacity.

Energy flow and mobility is distribution and freedom of transmission.

---

### What It Means in Practice

  

When my energy flow and mobility are clean:

- power moves through my body without joints locking
    
- tension redistributes instead of accumulating
    
- movement feels fluid instead of forced
    
- breath and structure cooperate
    
- I feel responsive, not heavy or stuck
    

  

When they’re compromised:

- force gets trapped in joints or fascia
    
- stiffness replaces stability
    
- movement becomes inefficient
    
- fatigue builds even if energy exists
    
- power leaks instead of transferring
    

---

### Role in My Architecture

  

This node is not about how much energy I have.

It’s about whether energy can circulate and express.

  

It tells me:

- if my structure is conductive or resistant
    
- if my fascia is elastic or congested
    
- if my movement patterns support power or block it
    
- if I’m fluid or fragmented in motion
    

  

Energy can exist without mobility.

Mobility without flow creates collapse.

This node holds the relationship.

---

### How I Experience It

  

I experience strong energy flow and mobility as:

- elasticity in my body
    
- smooth transitions between positions
    
- less muscular effort for more output
    
- natural recovery after exertion
    
- a feeling of internal circulation
    

  

When it’s low, I feel like power has nowhere to go.
