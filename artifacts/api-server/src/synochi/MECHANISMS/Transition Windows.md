Definition

My transition windows are the brief moments between states where my structure is shifting and influence can enter with the least resistance.

  

They’re not stable points.

They’re gaps in stability.

---

### What It Means In My System

  

I’m noticing that:

  

• systems aren’t most vulnerable when weak

• they’re vulnerable when changing

• stability has rigidity

• collapse has chaos

• transition has openness

  

Transition windows are where

my organisation hasn’t locked yet.

---

### How I Experience Them

  

I recognise my transition windows when:

  

• I’m shifting posture

• my breath changes direction

• I reset between movements

• I hesitate before acting

• my attention shifts focus

• my nervous system moves between states

• pressure feels like it “lands” more easily

  

They feel subtle, but exposed.

---

### Why They Matter

  

Transition windows are where:

  

• deep transmission happens

• structural timing becomes effective

• force doesn’t need to be forced

• influence becomes efficient

• state change is possible

• reactions can be rewritten

• adaptation happens without resistance

  

If I miss the window,

everything becomes effort.

---

### How I Work With Them

  

Instead of trying to force control, I:

  

• slow myself slightly

• become sensitive to micro-pauses

• feel when my structure is unsettled

• avoid rushing through transitions

• stay relaxed during resets

• allow perception to widen

• insert intention during openings

  

I don’t push.

I enter when open.
