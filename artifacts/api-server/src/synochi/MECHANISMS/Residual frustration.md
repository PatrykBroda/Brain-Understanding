**Residual frustration**

Residual frustration is the leftover charge after something is “over”, not the thing itself.

It’s not the main emotion.
It’s what remains when the surface emotion has already passed.

It shows up when:
	•	An experience ends without full completion
	•	I performed but didn’t fully express
	•	Something went “fine” but didn’t feel aligned
	•	My standards weren’t met even if the outcome was acceptable
	•	Energy had no clean exit

It feels like:
	•	A low-level irritation with no clear target
	•	Tightness behind the sternum or jaw
	•	Restlessness without direction
	•	A sense of “that wasn’t finished properly”
	•	An urge to move, correct, or re-do something

Residual frustration is not stored sympathetic energy.

Stored sympathetic energy is **paused survival output**.
Residual frustration is **misaligned or incomplete expression**.

Discharged energy feels like a release.
Residual frustration feels like an after-taste.

Discharge empties the system.
Residual frustration signals that something stayed unresolved or partially expressed.

Residual frustration reduces when:
	•	I acknowledge it instead of dismissing it
	•	I move my body or change state
	•	I reframe the situation cleanly without emotional repression
	•	I allow the system a cleaner end point (movement, breath, cold, rest)
	•	I stop pretending something felt “fine” when it didn’t

It increases when:
	•	I ignore it
	•	I mentally rationalise over it
	•	I suppress expression
	•	I force closure instead of letting it land

Residual frustration isn’t a failure.
It’s a signal that a loop didn’t close properly.

It’s not asking for drama.
It’s asking for accuracy.
