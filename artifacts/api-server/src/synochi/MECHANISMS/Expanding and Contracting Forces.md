# Expanding and Contracting Forces

**Definition:** A fundamental dynamic underlying states, cognition, and movement. Expansion opens perception, increases range, and invites options. Contraction compresses focus, increases force, and collapses choices. Mastery is not “always relaxed” — it is the ability to expand and contract on command.

**Mechanism:**
- Expansion = exploration, sensing, integration
- Contraction = execution, pressure, commitment
