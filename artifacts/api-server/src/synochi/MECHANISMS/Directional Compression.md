# Node: Directional Compression

  

Mechanism

Directional compression gathers internal force along a chosen vector — upward, inward, or forward — condensing energy instead of letting it disperse. This creates internal pressure that stabilises arousal, sharpens intention, and strengthens structural integrity.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel the system tighten in a clean, unified way without gripping. Breath becomes denser, posture lifts, and focus sharpens. When compression fails, energy spreads outward, creating leakiness or emotional spill.

  

Instruction

When I need clarity or power, compress energy along one direction and let the whole system organise around that internal pressure.
