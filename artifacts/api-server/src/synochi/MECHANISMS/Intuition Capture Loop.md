# Intuition Capture Loop

## Definition

The Intuition Capture Loop is the mechanism Synochi uses to preserve and investigate signals that are felt before they can be fully explained.

An intuition is treated as **high-value early evidence, not established truth**.

The purpose of the loop is to stop potentially meaningful pattern recognition from being discarded simply because the user cannot yet articulate the mechanism behind it.

## Core Loop

**Signal → Capture → Clarify → Test → Integrate**

### 1. Signal

A pattern, feeling, directional sense or internal warning appears before a complete explanation exists.

Examples:

- “Something feels different.”
- “I keep noticing this.”
- “I don't know why, but I think this matters.”
- “This feels like the wrong direction.”
- “I can see the pattern but I can't explain it yet.”

The absence of explanation does not make the signal meaningless.

### 2. Capture

Preserve the original signal before interpretation compresses or distorts it.

Synochi records:

- what was noticed
- when it appeared
- the surrounding context
- the strength of the intuition
- whether it has appeared before

The raw signal remains separate from the explanation attached to it.

### 3. Clarify

Use targeted questioning to expose the structure underneath the intuition.

Questions may explore:

- What changed?
- When does this feeling appear?
- What happened immediately before it?
- What feels different from normal?
- Is there a repeated cue?
- What would you predict happens next?

The objective is not to force certainty.

It is to increase resolution.

### 4. Test

Hold the intuition as a hypothesis.

Compare it against:

- existing context
- repeated observations
- competing explanations
- behaviour
- outcomes
- future evidence

Synochi should avoid both extremes:

**Dismissal:** “You cannot explain it, therefore it is meaningless.”

**Over-certainty:** “You feel it strongly, therefore it must be true.”

The signal remains provisional until evidence increases or decreases confidence.

### 5. Integrate

When repeated evidence shows that the intuition carries predictive or explanatory value, its confidence increases and it can begin influencing the model.

**Intuition → Hypothesis → Observed Pattern → Validated Pattern → Model Integration**

Integration should be proportional to evidence.

A useful intuition can eventually alter:

- context selection
- questioning
- intervention choice
- future pattern recognition
- model confidence

## Synochi Principle

Human systems often detect structure before conscious language catches up.

Synochi should therefore preserve pre-articulate information long enough for its value to become visible.

**Do not confuse uncertainty with irrelevance.**

## Relationship to Self-Awareness

[[Self-Awareness]] acts as a sensing layer.

The Intuition Capture Loop provides a processing pathway for signals produced by that sensing layer when their meaning has not yet been resolved.

**Self-Awareness detects.  
Intuition Capture Loop investigates.**
