## Related Nodes

**1. Abstract Thinking**

I don’t stay trapped at surface level.
I naturally move beyond what’s obvious and look for underlying meaning, structure, and principle.

This lets me:
	•	work with concepts instead of just events
	•	extract lessons instead of just experiences
	•	think in systems rather than isolated moments

⸻

**2. Conceptual Linking**

I connect ideas across different domains and experiences.

For example:
linking inevitability, containment, nervous system work, presence, training, and identity.

This means:
	•	my thinking isn’t fragmented
	•	I build frameworks, not just opinions
	•	I create coherence across different areas of life

⸻

**3. Meta-Awareness**

I don’t just think — I observe **how I think**.

This is the ability to step back and notice:
	•	my internal processes
	•	my emotional bias
	•	my cognitive patterns
	•	how states affect reasoning

This allows correction, refinement, and self-regulation at a higher level.

⸻

**4. Pattern Recognition**

I quickly notice:
	•	repeating behavioural patterns
	•	structural similarities across situations
	•	cause-and-effect relationships
	•	subtle shifts in states and outcomes

This helps me anticipate, adapt, and connect things before they become obvious to others.

⸻

**Core Line for Notes**

My intelligence is not defined by a number, but by my ability to think abstractly, link concepts, observe my own thinking, and recognise patterns across systems and experiences.
