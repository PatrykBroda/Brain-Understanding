Definition

My vagal tone is the strength and responsiveness of my vagus nerve in regulating heart rate, breath, and internal safety signals.

  

It reflects how easily my system can return to calm, coherence, and connection after activation.

---

### What It Means In My System

  

High vagal tone means:

  

• I recover faster after stress

• my baseline feels calmer

• my heart and breath synchronise more easily

• my social engagement stays available

• my system promotes safety, not threat

  

Low vagal tone means:

  

• I stay activated longer

• my stress lingers

• my breath feels shallow

• my recovery window narrows

• my threat bias increases

  

It’s basically the elasticity of my nervous system.

---

### How I Experience It

  

When my vagal tone is strong:

  

• my breath feels smooth and deep

• my chest feels open, not tight

• my heart rate settles quickly after exertion

• I can enter parasympathetic states easily

• my voice and face feel more relaxed

• I recover faster after BJJ rounds or stress

  

When it’s weak:

  

• I feel stuck in activation

• I struggle to downshift

• my jaw and face stay tense

• my sleep becomes lighter

• my aftershock phases last longer

---

### Relationship To My Framework

  

My vagal tone directly influences:

  

• parasympathetic regulation

• autonomic regulation

• threat sensitivity

• buffer zone stability

• motor tension level

• perception reorganisation

• aftershock recovery

• nervous system recalibration

• social engagement capacity

  

It’s one of the core physiological regulators of my entire architecture.

---

### How I Strengthen It

  

I strengthen my vagal tone through:

  

• resonant breathing

• slow exhale emphasis

• nasal breathing

• cold exposure followed by warming

• humming or vocal vibration

• facial and jaw relaxation

• consistent sleep rhythms

• safe social connection

• slow spinal wave movement

  

It strengthens through safety + repetition.

---

### Why This Node Matters

  

Vagal tone tells me:

  

• how resilient my nervous system really is

• how fast I recover from pressure

• how safe my body feels internally

• how much of my system is in survival mode

• how much access I have to coherence

  

It’s basically my nervous system’s resting strength.
