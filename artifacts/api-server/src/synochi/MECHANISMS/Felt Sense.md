Definition

My felt sense is my immediate, pre-verbal bodily knowing — the subtle, intuitive signal that tells me how something truly lands in my system before I analyse it.

  

It’s not a thought.

It’s information in sensation.

---

### What It Means In My System

  

My felt sense is the layer of perception that sits beneath language.

  

It’s where:

  

• my body registers truth or misalignment

• my nervous system signals safety or threat

• decisions are felt before they’re reasoned

• integration reveals itself before understanding

• coherence shows up as sensation, not concept

  

It’s the first place my system speaks.

---

### How I Experience It

  

When I tune into my felt sense:

  

• my chest or stomach gives me signals

• my posture subtly shifts

• certain ideas feel heavy or light

• some choices feel clean, others distorted

• tension, relaxation, or clarity appear without explanation

  

It doesn’t shout.

It whispers.

  

If I ignore it,

my system gets louder later.

---

### Felt Sense vs Emotion

  

Emotion is expressive.

Felt sense is informational.

  

Emotion reacts.

Felt sense informs.

  

Emotion has narrative.

Felt sense is raw signal.

  

When I confuse them, I overthink.

When I separate them, my decisions become cleaner.

---

### Relationship To My Architecture

  

My felt sense links directly with:

  

• my somatic awareness

• my sensory anchoring

• my nervous system landscape

• my threshold dynamics

• my perception reorganisation

• my central axis alignment

• my system agency

• my response capacity

  

It’s a critical input layer in my whole system.

---

### How I Strengthen My Felt Sense

  

I refine it by:

  

• slowing down before reacting

• bringing attention into my body

• staying with sensations instead of analysing them

• noticing micro-shifts in tension or openness

• using the breath to open body awareness

• tracking subtle internal changes over time

  

Felt sense strengthens in stillness and presence.

---

### Why This Node Matters

  

My felt sense is the earliest detection system I have.

  

It detects:

  

• distortion

• misalignment

• truth

• danger

• coherence

• integration

• internal contradiction

  

Without it, I live conceptually.

With it, my system stays embodied and accurate.
