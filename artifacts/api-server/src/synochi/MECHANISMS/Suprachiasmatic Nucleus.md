### Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN)

  

The Suprachiasmatic Nucleus is a tiny cluster of neurons in my hypothalamus that acts as my master circadian clock.

  

It decides:

• when I feel sleepy

• when I feel alert

• when melatonin gets released

• when cortisol rises

• how my body syncs with light and darkness

  

It doesn’t produce melatonin itself.

It controls when melatonin is released by sending signals to the pineal gland.

  

Light hits my eyes → signals travel to SCN → SCN tells pineal gland what time it is → melatonin goes up or down.

  

So yes:

It’s basically the timekeeper that schedules melatonin.
