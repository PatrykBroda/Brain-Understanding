# Node: Predictive Delay

  

Mechanism

Predictive delay is the gap between my system recognising a pattern and my body executing the response. When the delay is clean, action feels immediate and precise. When it widens, hesitation, overthinking, or mis-timing appear.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel slight lag in decisions, late reactions in transitions, or tension building before the body commits. My perception is ahead of my movement.

  

Instruction

When I sense predictive delay, shorten the gap by anchoring breath, reducing unnecessary tension, and committing earlier to the recognised pattern.
