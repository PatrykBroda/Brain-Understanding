I see my system as having gates, not walls.

  

Not walls that block.

Not filters that reject.

  

Gates that change state based on charge.

  

Voltage-gated permeability is how I understand the way my nervous system decides

what gets entry,

what gets routed,

and what stays inactive.

  

Not through judgement.

Through thresholds.

---

### Core Definition

  

My permeability is not fixed.

It’s voltage dependent.

  

The level of my internal arousal, charge, or tension determines:

• what enters

• what is amplified

• what gets ignored

• what gets re-routed

  

Just like ion channels:

They don’t “choose” to open.

They respond to electrical state.

  

So do I.

---

### The Mechanism (Conceptual)

  

In my framework, voltage-gated permeability is the transition layer between:

  

• raw sensory input

• primitive system activation

• higher cortical processing

• and my central axis of control

  

It’s the gateway between stimulus and meaning.

  

Not a filter.

More like a routing switchboard.

  

When my system is calm and coherent:

→ my gates open selectively

→ I perceive clearly

→ information flows with alignment

  

When over-stimulated or ego-defensive:

→ my voltage spikes

→ gates become chaotic or restrictive

→ routing becomes distorted

  

This is not moral.

It’s electrical.

---

### Permeability as Routing, Not Blocking

  

I’m not trying to block reality.

  

I’m directing how it moves through me.

  

Permeability isn’t defence.

It’s directional access control.

  

My system opens towards what my structure can integrate,

and redirects what it can’t.

  

This makes permeability a navigation mechanism, not a barrier.

  

A door, not a guard.

Primitive system hijacking isn’t forceful takeover.

  

It’s voltage override.

  

When emotional charge exceeds my regulatory capacity:

Control isn’t lost.
