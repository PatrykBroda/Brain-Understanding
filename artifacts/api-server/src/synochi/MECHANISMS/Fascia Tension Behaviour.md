Mechanism

My fascia sets the direction and quality of internal tension. It carries load, redirects force, and determines how energy travels through my structure. When fascia engages cleanly, power conducts smoothly; when it grips or collapses, signal distortion rises.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel lines of tension running through spirals, diagonals, and recoil patterns rather than isolated muscle effort. Misalignment shows up as gripping, sticking, or loss of elasticity.

  

Instruction

When tension feels chaotic or force leaks, tune into the fascia lines and let them organise the load before applying power.
