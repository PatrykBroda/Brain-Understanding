Definition

My jaw tension is the baseline holding pattern in my jaw muscles that reflects my nervous system’s current level of threat, containment, and readiness.

  

It’s not just muscular.

It’s a somatic readout of my internal state.

---

### Why My Jaw Matters So Much

  

My jaw sits at a crossroads between:

  

• threat detection

• emotional expression

• breath pattern

• cranial nerve signalling

• vagal tone

• facial tension

• postural stability

  

So when my jaw is tight,

my entire nervous system is usually in some level of defence.

  

When it softens,

it’s often the first sign of safety or regulation.

---

### What Jaw Tension Means In My System

  

When my jaw is tense:

  

• my threat sensitivity is elevated

• my buffer zone shrinks

• my face contracts

• my breathing becomes shallow or high

• my perception narrows

• my body prepares for defence or action

  

When my jaw is relaxed:

  

• my system feels safer

• my breath moves lower

• my face softens

• my central axis feels clearer

• my reactions slow down

• my internal space opens

  

It’s not just a muscle condition.

It’s a nervous system signal.

---

### How I Use Jaw Tension As a Signal

  

I treat jaw tension as a diagnostic tool, not just a problem.

  

It tells me:

  

• how contained I am

• how threatened I feel

• whether I’m reactive or regulated

• whether I’m integrated or bracing

• whether my system is in aftershock or stability

  

It becomes a live sensor for my internal state.

---

### How I Work With It

  

I don’t force my jaw to relax.

I observe it.

  

Then I adjust:

  

• breath

• neck alignment

• posture

• facial tone

• attention location

  

If it releases naturally, my system is ready.

If it doesn’t, my system still needs containment.
