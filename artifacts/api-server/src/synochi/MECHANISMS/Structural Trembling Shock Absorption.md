# Node: Structural Trembling / Shock Absorption

  

Mechanism

Structural trembling appears when the system encounters new force patterns it hasn’t stabilised yet. The shaking is the body mapping load pathways, building elasticity, and upgrading force distribution — not a sign of weakness, but active recalibration.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel micro-shakes, wobble, pulsing tension, or rapid adjustments through my legs, hips, or spine. The system is learning how to absorb and redirect force without collapsing.

  

Instruction

When trembling shows up, stay relaxed, breathe low, and let the structure adapt instead of bracing or shutting it down.
