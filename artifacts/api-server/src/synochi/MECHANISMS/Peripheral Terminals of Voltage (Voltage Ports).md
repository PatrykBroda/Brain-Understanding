In my model, my nerve endings are not just “sensors” —

they are my peripheral terminals of voltage.

  

They are the physical ports where external reality plugs into my nervous system.

  

Every touch, pressure, temperature shift, vibration, impact or presence first enters me here —

as electrical potential, not meaning.

  

They don’t interpret.

They don’t judge.

They transmit raw charge.

  

I see them as voltage ports, not defences.

Not bodyguards.

Just access points.

  

They feed directly into my permeability system, where routing begins.

  

This is where:

- the external interfaces with the internal
    
- raw sensation becomes neural currency
    
- voltage becomes direction
    
- and signal becomes possibility
    

  

Their job isn’t to protect me.

Their job is to deliver packets of reality into my system.

  

What happens next is determined by:

my containment,

my arousal state,

my central orientation,

and my regulatory structure.

  

As my system becomes more coherent,

I gain choice over how voltage moves beyond these ports —

instead of being hijacked at the entrance.

---

### Function in my architecture

  

Peripheral voltage ports:

- Transduce physical stimulus → electrical signal
    
- Feed raw charge into [[Voltage Gated Permeability]]
    
- Interface with [[Buffer Zones]] before signal reaches identity
    
- Provide entry points for both primitive and higher systems
    
- Act as terrain contact points for [[Somatic Grounding]]
    
- Influence signal load on [[Arousal Regulation]]
    
- Are potential hijack points for [[Primitive System Hijacking]]
    
- Are stabilised through [[Identity Containment]] and [[Perceptual Sovereignty]]
