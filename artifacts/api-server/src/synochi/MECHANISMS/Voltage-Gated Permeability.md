Definition

My voltage-gated permeability is how my system biologically decides when to open or close its boundaries based on internal or external charge.

  

It’s the mechanism behind how porous or protected I am at any moment.

---

### What It Means in My Experience

  

When my voltage-gated permeability is balanced:

  

• I feel open but not exposed

• stimulation enters without overwhelming me

• I don’t feel rigid or porous

• information flows without flooding

• my boundaries feel responsive, not defensive

  

When it’s distorted:

  

• I feel either too open or completely shut

• everything overwhelms me or nothing reaches me

• my system swings between hyper-reactive and numb

• boundaries feel unsafe or rigid

• internal space feels unstable

---

### How It Relates to My System

  

This governs:

  

• how stimulus crosses into my system

• how much energy and information passes through me

• whether my Buffer Zone can function properly

• how safe openness feels

• how regulated my internal space is

  

It’s the micro-level gatekeeper beneath my experience of space and containment.

  

Voltage-gated permeability isn’t emotional in itself,

but it shapes how emotional and sensory input reaches me.

---

### How I Notice It

  

I experience it through:

  

• how easily I take things in

• how strongly things affect me

• how quickly I feel overloaded

• how porous or resistant I feel

• how safe or unsafe openness feels

  

When it’s working well, I feel selectively permeable.

Not open. Not closed.

Just regulated.
