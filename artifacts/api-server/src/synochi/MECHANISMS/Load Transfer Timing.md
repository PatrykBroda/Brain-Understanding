# Node: Load-Transfer Timing (Owen’s Principle)

  

Mechanism

Load-transfer timing determines whether force enters the system when my structure is ready. When timing matches structure, energy flows cleanly. When load arrives too early or too late, the system leaks, braces, or collapses.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel smooth, connected force when timing is right. When it’s off, I notice jolts, over-gripping, hesitation, or sudden instability in hips, spine, or breath.

  

Instruction

When receiving or generating force, match the timing to structural readiness — stabilise the axis first, then accept or apply load.
