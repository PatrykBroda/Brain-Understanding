Definition

Conduction of power is how force, intention, and energy move through me without leakage.

It’s the quality of transmission between centre, structure, and action.

  

It’s not how much power I have.

It’s how cleanly it travels.

---

### What It Means In My System

  

When my conduction of power is clean:

- force moves without collapse or distortion
    
- effort feels minimal but effective
    
- direction holds under pressure
    
- structure supports output instead of fighting it
    
- I don’t muscle power — it arrives
    

  

When it’s poor:

- energy leaks
    
- movement feels forced
    
- I overcompensate
    
- tension replaces transmission
    
- direction fractures under load
    

---

### How I Experience It

  

I feel it as:

- alignment between intention and movement
    
- reduced internal friction
    
- quieter effort with stronger effect
    
- no break between decision and execution
    
- power travelling through, not being pushed out
    

  

It’s not aggression.

It’s coherence under force.

---

### Why It Matters

  

Conduction of power sits between:

  

• intention

• structure

• action

• environment

  

If this is weak, every other system compensates.

If this is strong, everything downstream simplifies.

  

It is the efficiency layer of my entire architecture.
