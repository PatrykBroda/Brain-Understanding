
[[Escape Priority]]
[[Combat Intelligence Hub]]

Large positional escapes are often built through smaller recoveries that progressively restore freedom of movement.

Examples:

- freeing the head
- recovering one elbow
- clearing one knee
- removing one hook
- turning one shoulder
- rebuilding knee-elbow connection

The athlete does not always need to escape the entire position immediately.

Often the first priority is to recover one piece of structure that makes the next movement possible.

Synochi should recognise whether the athlete is progressively improving the defensive position rather than judging success only by whether full guard, standing, or another neutral position has already been recovered.

Escape quality can therefore be measured by whether each movement creates a better defensive structure and opens the next layer of recovery.
