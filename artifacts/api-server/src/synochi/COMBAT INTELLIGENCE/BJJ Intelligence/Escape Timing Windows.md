
[[Escape Priority]]
[[Combat Intelligence Hub]]

Escapes are often timing-dependent rather than continuously available.

A control position may briefly weaken when the top athlete:

- changes grips
- transitions between pins
- attacks a submission
- shifts weight
- posts to maintain balance
- follows a previous escape attempt

These moments create escape windows.

Synochi should distinguish between:

mechanical possibility

and

timing opportunity.

A technically correct escape attempted against settled structure may fail, while the same movement may succeed during a transition.

Coaching should therefore identify when the opponent's control is temporarily reorganising.
