
[[Half Guard]]
[[Combat Intelligence Hub]]

Ruleset:
Both

Ruleset Notes:

- Gi: belt and fabric grips can strengthen control around the waist and hips.
- No Gi: relies more heavily on body connection, waist control, hip position, and rotational pressure.

Octopus Guard is a Half Guard configuration where the bottom athlete comes around the opponent’s back or side, usually using a deep waist or body connection while staying connected to the trapped leg.

It should be understood as a rotational control and wrestle up position rather than simply a variation of Half Guard.

Primary bottom player objectives include:

- get around the opponent’s upper body rather than remaining square underneath
- establish strong connection around the waist or torso
- keep control of the trapped leg
- prevent the opponent from turning fully back toward the bottom athlete
- build height
- disrupt the opponent’s base
- create rotational pressure
- convert the position into a sweep, wrestle up, or back exposure

Common attacking pathways include:

- wrestle up
- hip bump style reversal
- back exposure
- rear body lock
- single leg finish
- transition to Dogfight
- transition to standing
- sweep to top position

Primary top player objectives include:

- turn back toward the bottom athlete
- deny the waist or body connection
- flatten the bottom athlete
- free the trapped leg
- control the head and shoulders
- prevent the bottom athlete from building height
- redirect into chest to chest Half Guard or a passing position

Key mechanical variables include:

- depth of waist connection
- hip angle
- trapped leg control
- ability to get around the opponent’s side
- head position
- shoulder position
- rotational control
- ability to build onto elbow, hand, or knee

Video observation cues:

- bottom athlete reaching around the waist without controlling rotation
- strong body connection preventing the opponent from squaring
- athlete successfully building height from the position
- opponent turning back in and collapsing the angle
- rotational pressure exposing the back
- transition from Octopus Guard into a wrestle up or rear body lock
- trapped leg being freed before upper body control is established

Synochi should recognise Octopus Guard as a rotational Half Guard system.

Its effectiveness comes from getting around the opponent’s side, maintaining strong body connection, and using that positional relationship to build height and attack the opponent’s base rather than remaining underneath chest to chest pressure.
