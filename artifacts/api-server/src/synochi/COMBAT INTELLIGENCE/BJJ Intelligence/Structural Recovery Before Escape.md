

[[Escape Priority]]
[[Combat Intelligence Hub]]

Large escape movement becomes inefficient when the athlete remains structurally compromised.

Useful structural recovery may include:

- recovering elbow position
- restoring head-spine alignment
- freeing a shoulder
- changing pelvic angle
- reconnecting feet to the floor
- rebuilding knee-elbow relationship

Synochi should identify whether the athlete first needs to restore usable structure before attempting positional movement.
