# Arm Triangle Finishing Schema

## Definition

Arm Triangle Finishing is a strangulation finishing structure in which the opponent's own shoulder and the attacker's arm compress both sides of the neck.

The finish depends on alignment and shoulder positioning rather than raw squeezing force.

## Primary Objective

Position the opponent's shoulder against one side of the neck while the attacker's arm closes the opposite side.

## Core Mechanics

1. Move the opponent's arm across the neck.
2. Control head position.
3. Remove space between the opponent's shoulder and neck.
4. Establish deep arm position.
5. Align the attacker's torso with the finishing direction.
6. Apply progressive shoulder and chest pressure.
7. Maintain position as the opponent attempts to create space.
8. Finish through structural compression rather than rapid squeezing.

## Key Principle

**The opponent's shoulder is part of the choke.**

If the shoulder is not positioned correctly, additional squeezing usually compensates poorly.

## Decision Rules

If space remains near the neck:

- improve shoulder position before increasing force

If the opponent turns:

- follow the head and shoulder alignment

If the arm begins slipping back:

- restore arm position before continuing

If the structure is correct:

- apply pressure progressively

## Failure Modes

- squeezing before the shoulder is positioned
- leaving space between neck and shoulder
- poor head control
- incorrect torso alignment
- using arm strength instead of structural pressure

## Gi / No-Gi Distinction

The core finishing mechanics remain similar.

**Gi:** fabric can affect friction and head movement.

**No-Gi:** chest pressure and positional alignment are more exposed and therefore more important.

## Synochi Read

Arm Triangle Finishing is an **alignment-dependent compression schema**.

Finishing probability increases as unnecessary space approaches zero.
