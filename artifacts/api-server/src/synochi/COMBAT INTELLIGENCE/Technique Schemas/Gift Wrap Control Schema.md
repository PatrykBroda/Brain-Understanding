# Gift Wrap Control Schema

## Definition

Gift Wrap Control is an upper-body control structure in which one of the opponent's arms is carried across their own torso and secured, reducing their ability to frame, rotate or defend the back.

## Primary Objective

Create upper-body asymmetry and use the trapped arm to control shoulder rotation.

## Core Mechanics

1. Bring the opponent's arm across their torso.
2. Secure the wrist or forearm.
3. Maintain chest or shoulder connection.
4. Prevent the opponent from retracting the arm.
5. Use the trapped arm to control rotation.
6. Progress toward Mount, Back Control or another dominant position.
7. Maintain the control during transition.
8. Release only after the new position is secure.

## Key Principle

**The opponent's own arm becomes part of the control structure.**

## Decision Rules

If the opponent turns away:

- use the trapped arm to expose the back

If the opponent turns toward the passer:

- maintain shoulder control and preserve the pin

If the arm begins escaping:

- prioritise re-securing control before transitioning

If the back becomes available:

- maintain upper-body connection during the transition

## Failure Modes

- holding the wrist without controlling the shoulder
- allowing the elbow to retract
- transitioning before the opponent's rotation is controlled
- losing chest connection
- releasing the arm too early

## Gi / No-Gi Distinction

**Gi:** sleeve control can reinforce the structure.

**No-Gi:** wrist control, elbow positioning and chest connection become more important.

## Synochi Read

Gift Wrap Control is an **internal asymmetry schema**.

The opponent's defensive limb is converted into a mechanism that restricts their own rotation.
