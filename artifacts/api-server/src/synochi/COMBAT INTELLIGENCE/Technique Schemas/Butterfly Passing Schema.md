# Butterfly Passing Schema

## Definition

Butterfly Passing is the process of neutralising the opponent's seated or supine hook-based guard by reducing the effectiveness of their hooks, controlling their hip orientation and progressing beyond the knees.

## Primary Objective

Prevent the opponent's hooks from creating elevation, rotation or meaningful separation.

## Entry Conditions

The schema becomes available when:

- the opponent has established Butterfly Guard
- one or both hooks can be controlled or made ineffective
- the passer can maintain stable base
- the opponent's upper-body connection can be managed

## Core Mechanics

1. Stabilise base.
2. Identify the active hook.
3. Reduce the hook's ability to elevate.
4. Control the opponent's knees or hips.
5. Prevent effective reorientation.
6. Collapse or bypass the hook structure.
7. Advance beyond the knees.
8. Consolidate control.

## Key Principle

**A butterfly hook matters only while it can meaningfully influence the passer's centre of mass.**

Neutralise the hook's mechanical relationship to the passer before progressing.

## Decision Rules

If the opponent elevates:

- widen or relocate base and reduce the active hook

If the opponent sits upright:

- manage upper-body connection before advancing

If one hook disappears:

- exploit the asymmetry immediately

If the opponent transitions into another guard:

- follow the positional transition rather than forcing the old pass

## Failure Modes

- driving weight directly onto active hooks
- allowing both hooks to remain mechanically useful
- narrowing the base unnecessarily
- ignoring upper-body connection
- progressing without monitoring the opponent's hip rotation

## Gi / No-Gi Distinction

**Gi:** grips can limit upper-body movement and leg recovery.

**No-Gi:** base, head position and close body connection become more important.

## Synochi Read

Butterfly Passing is a **load-management problem**.

The opponent's hooks become dangerous when they can meaningfully redirect the passer's weight.

Passing begins by reducing that influence.
