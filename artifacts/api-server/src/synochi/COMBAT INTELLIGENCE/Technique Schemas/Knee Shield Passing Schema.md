# Knee Shield Passing Schema

## Definition

Knee Shield Passing is the process of neutralising the structural barrier created by the opponent's knee between the passer and their torso.

The knee shield must first lose its ability to manage distance and angle before meaningful pressure passing can occur.

## Primary Objective

Remove or bypass the shield while preventing the opponent from replacing it with another effective frame.

## Entry Conditions

The schema becomes available when:

- the opponent is using a knee shield from Half Guard
- the passer can control distance around the shield
- access exists to the opponent's upper or lower body
- the shield can be compressed, redirected or bypassed

## Core Mechanics

1. Identify the orientation of the knee shield.
2. Prevent the shield from extending effectively.
3. Control the opponent's hip angle.
4. Compress, redirect or bypass the knee.
5. Close the space previously maintained by the shield.
6. Establish upper-body connection.
7. Progress beyond the defensive knee.
8. Consolidate the pass.

## Key Principle

**The knee shield is primarily a distance-management structure.**

Do not attack only the knee.

Attack the space and angle it is protecting.

## Decision Rules

If the shield is extended:

- reduce its usable range before entering

If the knee collapses inward:

- occupy the newly available space

If the opponent retracts the shield:

- close distance before it can be rebuilt

If the opponent changes hip angle:

- adjust passing direction rather than forcing the original line

## Failure Modes

- driving directly into a structurally strong shield
- controlling the knee without controlling the hip
- leaving space after collapsing the shield
- allowing the knee to return inside
- attempting pressure before resolving distance

## Gi / No-Gi Distinction

**Gi:** gripping can help immobilise the shield and far leg.

**No-Gi:** wedges, head position and bodyweight must perform more of the control.

## Synochi Read

Knee Shield Passing is a **barrier-resolution problem**.

The shield controls access by regulating distance.

Remove its structural function and the passing environment changes immediately.
