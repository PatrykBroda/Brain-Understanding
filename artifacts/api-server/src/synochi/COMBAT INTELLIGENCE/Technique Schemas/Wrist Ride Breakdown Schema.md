# Wrist Ride Breakdown Schema

## Definition

Wrist Ride Breakdown is a control structure used primarily against Turtle or partially seated opponents in which wrist control is used to distort posture, deny posting and create rotational access.

## Primary Objective

Use control of the wrist and arm to remove a key defensive post and expose the opponent's torso or back.

## Core Mechanics

1. Secure wrist control.
2. Keep the controlled arm separated from effective posting position.
3. Apply directional pressure through the shoulder line.
4. Control the hips or torso with the opposite side.
5. Create rotation.
6. Prevent the wrist from returning underneath the opponent.
7. Follow the resulting opening.
8. Transition into Back Control, Side Control or another dominant structure.

## Key Principle

**The wrist is valuable because it controls a post.**

Removing the post changes the opponent's whole defensive structure.

## Decision Rules

If the opponent retracts the elbow:

- maintain wrist control and follow the shoulder

If the opponent posts with the opposite arm:

- redirect rotational pressure

If the opponent begins standing:

- maintain rear control and transition accordingly

If the wrist becomes inaccessible:

- move to another controlling structure rather than forcing it

## Failure Modes

- controlling the wrist without controlling the shoulder
- allowing the arm to return underneath the opponent
- applying force without directional purpose
- ignoring hip movement
- becoming attached to the wrist after the position changes

## Gi / No-Gi Distinction

**Gi:** sleeve grips can increase persistence.

**No-Gi:** mechanical wrist control and elbow positioning become more important.

## Synochi Read

Wrist Ride Breakdown is a **post-removal schema**.

One controlled limb can alter the stability of the entire defensive system.
