# Single Leg X Passing Schema

## Definition

Single Leg X Passing is the process of dismantling the opponent's control around one isolated leg before crossing the defensive leg line.

The passer must restore alignment and remove the opponent's control of the trapped leg before attempting unrestricted movement.

## Primary Objective

Recover ownership of the trapped leg while preventing the opponent from maintaining effective control of distance, balance and rotation.

## Entry Conditions

The schema becomes available when:

- the opponent has established Single Leg X
- one leg is isolated
- the passer can maintain or recover balance
- the opponent's controlling feet and knees can be addressed

## Core Mechanics

1. Stabilise posture and base.
2. Identify the opponent's primary controlling structures.
3. Prevent further off-balancing.
4. Remove or neutralise the strongest control.
5. Restore alignment of the trapped knee and hip.
6. Clear the leg from the entanglement.
7. Prevent immediate re-entry.
8. Convert directly into passing pressure where possible.

## Key Principle

**Escape the control structure before attempting to pass the guard around it.**

## Decision Rules

If balance is compromised:

- prioritise base before stripping controls

If the opponent extends the trapped leg:

- restore knee and hip alignment

If a control is removed:

- occupy the resulting space before it is replaced

If the entanglement transitions:

- reassess the new positional state rather than continuing the old sequence

## Failure Modes

- attempting to step away while structurally trapped
- ignoring balance disruption
- stripping controls without preventing replacement
- leaving the knee line exposed
- clearing the leg but failing to prevent re-entry

## Gi / No-Gi Distinction

**Gi:** grips can increase the opponent's ability to maintain the position.

**No-Gi:** control is more dependent on leg configuration and body alignment.

## Synochi Read

Single Leg X Passing is a **control-removal sequence**.

**Stabilise → identify dominant control → remove constraint → restore alignment → pass**
