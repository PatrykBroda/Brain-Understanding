Stress isn’t the problem.

Mismanaged stress is.

  

There are two very different forms:

  

### Acute Stress

  

Acute stress is short, sharp, and purposeful.

  

It happens when:

• I spar

• I train

• I face a challenge

• I enter high-focus states

• I push cognitive or physical limits

  

In acute stress:

• adrenaline rises

• focus narrows appropriately

• reaction speed increases

• energy mobilises

• and then… it passes

  

When handled properly, acute stress is adaptive.

  

It sharpens my system.

It increases resilience.

It drives growth.

  

This is the type of stress that builds me.

---

### Chronic Stress

  

Chronic stress is low-to-mid level activation that never turns off.

  

It happens when:

• I don’t recover

• I stay mentally overstimulated

• I overanalyse without integrating

• I stay in vigilance mode

• I don’t close loops

  

In chronic stress:

• cortisol stays elevated

• sleep quality drops

• emotional reactivity increases

• recovery decreases

• and my baseline shifts downward

  

This doesn’t sharpen me.

It erodes me.

---

### The Real Difference

  

Acute stress:

→ Has a beginning and end

→ My system knows how to exit

  

Chronic stress:

→ Has no clear end

→ My system never feels safe enough to downshift

  

One builds capacity.

The other drains it.

---

### Why This Matters in My Work

  

Because I’m doing high-load nervous system work.

  

If I:

• stimulate without integrating

• train without recovery

• or spike arousal without down-regulating

  

I risk turning productive stress into chronic overload.

  

And then:

performance degrades

clarity blurs

and the map stops reflecting reality.

### My Rule

  

I want frequent acute stress

and minimal chronic stress.
