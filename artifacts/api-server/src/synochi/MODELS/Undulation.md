**Undulation**

Undulation is wave-like movement travelling through the body.

It’s not just flexibility.
It’s sequential spinal motion that carries energy from one end of the body to the other without interruption.

Undulation happens when:
	•	The spine moves as a chain instead of rigid segments
	•	The joints coordinate instead of isolating
	•	The body transitions through space instead of jumping between positions

It shows up in:
	•	BJJ transitions and inversions
	•	Breath-to-movement integration
	•	Nervous system discharge
	•	Expressive movement and posture shifts
	•	Subtle emotional releases through the body

When undulation is present:
	•	Movement feels continuous, not mechanical
	•	Energy distributes instead of getting stuck
	•	Power is transferred instead of forced
	•	Expression happens without strain
	•	The body feels like one system, not parts

When undulation is missing:
	•	Movement feels stiff or segmented
	•	Energy pools in the chest, neck or hips
	•	Expression gets trapped or delayed
	•	Transitions feel effortful instead of natural

Undulation allows energy to travel through the body without bottlenecks.

Breath initiates it.
The spine carries it.
The limbs express it.

In discharge work, undulation is how stored energy leaves without fragmentation.

In combat movement, undulation is how I maintain flow under pressure.

It’s the difference between:
Pushing through space
and
Moving through it.

Undulation isn’t a technique.
It’s a quality of movement.

And when it’s there, everything else works better.
