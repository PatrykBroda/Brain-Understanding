# Salience After Silence

## Definition
Salience After Silence is the ability to identify what is truly important **only after inputs drop**.

When noise reduces, the real signal becomes obvious.

---

## Core Law
**Salience appears when load drops.**

## Rules
- Don’t decide mid-stimulation.
- Reduce inputs, then re-check what matters.
- Treat “clarity after rest” as higher authority than “clarity while hyped”.

---

## Failure Mode
Confusing stimulation salience for true relevance.

---

## Spine Line
**Silence reveals priority.**
