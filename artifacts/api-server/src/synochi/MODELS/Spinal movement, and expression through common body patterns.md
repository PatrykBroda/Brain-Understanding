**Spinal movement, and expression through common body patterns**

The spine is the main highway for energy flow.
If the spine moves well, energy distributes.
If the spine is locked, energy backs up or spills out as tension.

Most blockages aren’t emotional.
They’re spinal.

Energy moves through the body along the spine’s ability to:
	•	Flex
	•	Extend
	•	Rotate
	•	Side-bend
	•	Undulate

If one of these is restricted, flow becomes segmented.

When the spine is mobile:
	•	Energy travels from head to pelvis without interruption
	•	Breath integrates with movement
	•	The body responds as one piece instead of parts
	•	Expression comes out proportionate and coordinated

When the spine is stiff or compressed:
	•	Energy gathers in the chest, neck, or jaw
	•	Movement becomes disconnected
	•	Expression becomes either muted or exaggerated
	•	The body tries to leak energy through smaller channels (face, hands, voice)

Common movement patterns in your work that affect flow:

Spinal flexion
Forward folding, rounding, curling postures.
This pulls energy inward.
If overused or locked → leads to chest compression and stored tension.

Spinal extension
Opening through the chest and thoracic spine.
This allows expansion and expression.
If blocked → emotions and energy get trapped in the upper body.

Spinal rotation
Twisting through the midline.
This clears held energy from the obliques and rib cage.
If limited → energy stagnates around the core and back.

Spinal undulation
Wave-like movement through the whole spine.
This is key for full discharge and integration.
If missing → energy releases in fragments instead of a full cycle.

Pelvic tilt and sacral movement
The pelvis governs whether energy can descend.
If the pelvis is locked, energy stays high and becomes anxiety or frustration.

Neck mobility
The neck acts as a valve between brain and body.
If tense, energy backs up into the head and face.

In visual and discharge exercises, spinal movement is what completes the loop.
It prevents energy from staying trapped in the chest or head.

Breath initiates movement.
The spine carries it.
The limbs express it.

If the spine is free, expression feels natural.
If the spine is restricted, expression feels forced, delayed, or incomplete.

Every clean discharge is a spinal discharge.
Even when it looks emotional.

The spine isn’t just posture.
It’s the main channel through which energy turns into motion, and motion turns into expression.
