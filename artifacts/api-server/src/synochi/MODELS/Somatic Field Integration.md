# Node: Somatic Field Integration

  

Mechanism

The somatic field is the full-body sensory map that unifies breath, tension, orientation, and emotional charge into one coherent space. When the field integrates, signals stop competing and the entire system operates as a single unit instead of fragmented zones.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel awareness spread through my torso, back, hips, and limbs as one connected field. When the field breaks, I notice pockets of numbness, over-sensitivity, or disconnection between intention and physical response.

  

Instruction

When I feel divided or scattered, widen awareness through the whole body and re-establish one continuous somatic field before acting.
