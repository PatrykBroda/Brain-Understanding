### Reaction

  

A reaction is automatic, fast, and driven by lower processing layers.

It happens before choice.

  

It’s:

- reflex-based
    
- threat-driven
    
- body-first
    
- often followed by rationalisation
    

  

Reaction is the system protecting itself without asking you.

---

### Response

  

A response happens after awareness.

It includes a pause, even if that pause is microscopic.

  

It’s:

- chosen, not triggered
    
- centrally directed
    
- informed by the whole system
    
- not driven by immediate threat bias
    

  

Response is me deciding instead of being moved.

---

### Key Difference

  

Reaction is something happening to me.

Response is something happening through me.

---

### Why It Matters

  

The gap between reaction and response

is where sovereignty lives.

  

No gap → I’m run by stimulus.

Gap present → I can direct my behaviour.

---

### Where the Gap Comes From

  

The gap is created by a stable internal command order.

When my system isn’t hijacked, it can hold space between stimulus and action.

That space is the difference.

---

### One Line

  

Reaction is automatic.

Response is intentional.
