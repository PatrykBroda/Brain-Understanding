
### Definition

  

Structural Mechanics is the study of how forces move through structure.

It explains why systems either collapse, absorb, or redirect load.

  

This is not metaphor.

It is the physics under everything else.

---

### Core Concepts

- Helical tension systems
    
- Spiral / vortex structural principles
    
- Expanding vs contracting forces
    
- Structural trembling & shock absorption
    
- Collapse → redirection mechanics
    
- Rotational load vs linear load
    
- Tessellating structural webs
    
- Axis / rotational pivots
    

---

### Models & Metaphors

- Helical Spring Model
    
    Structures survive by coiling, not bracing
    
    Elasticity over rigidity
    
- Collapse–Redirection Model
    
    Energy must be redirected, not resisted
    
- Structural Web Model
    
    Strength comes from distributed geometry
    
    Not single-point reinforcement
