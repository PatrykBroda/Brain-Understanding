Permeability isn’t my system being open or closed.

It’s how my system chooses where energy and information are sent.

  

It doesn’t act like a wall.

It behaves like a routing interface.

  

I’m not “letting things in” or “blocking things out”.

I’m directing flows into different internal territories depending on my state, identity containment and CNS coherence.

  

When my system is regulated:

• permeability becomes selective

• information is routed, not absorbed

• stimulation enters but doesn’t destabilise

• signals are redirected, not reacted to

  

When my system is dysregulated:

• permeability becomes chaotic

• everything hits everything

• emotional voltage floods the wrong circuits

• primitive hijacking overrides routing intelligence

  

Permeability is not defence.

  

It’s traffic control.

  

I don’t try to block experiences.

I train the system to decide where they land.

  

If I’m fragmented, everything goes everywhere.

If I’m integrated, flows are sorted.

### Buffer Zones

  

My buffer zones are neutral routing layers between stimulus and response.

  

They’re not delays.

They’re distribution chambers.

  

They let:

• intensity cool before processing

• emotion stabilise before identity engagement

• stimulus separate from meaning

• threat signals be classified instead of obeyed

  

They exist between:

trigger → reaction

stimulus → decision

input → identity

  

When these zones collapse, hijacking occurs.

### Permeability vs Blocking

  

Blocking is fear-based.

Permeability is structure-based.

  

Blocking makes me rigid.

Permeability makes me responsive.

  

A closed system shatters under impact.

A permeable system absorbs and re-routes.
