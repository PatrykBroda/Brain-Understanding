I understand my decision system as a macro version of voltage-gated activation.

  

At a biological level, a voltage gate decides when a neuron fires based on whether a threshold is reached.

Below threshold → nothing happens.

Beyond threshold → activation is released.

  

My decision architecture works the same way.

  

Every decision I make is the result of:

• internal signals accumulating

• pressure approaching a threshold

• and a gate either opening or staying closed

  

If my system is regulated, my thresholds are clean and intentional.

If my system is hijacked or fragmented, my thresholds become distorted — triggering too early, too late, or not at all.

  

So decision-making isn’t just psychology for me.

It’s threshold engineering inside my nervous system.

  

The stronger my perceptual control and containment,

the more precisely I control when something fires.

  

That’s not just about better choices.

It’s about governing activation itself.
