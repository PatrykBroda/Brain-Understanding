## Definition

  

Sleep Architecture is the structural organisation of sleep across the night — how different sleep stages are distributed, sequenced, and cycled.

  

Not just sleep time.

Sleep structure.

---

## Core Components

  

Sleep is organised into repeating 90–120 minute cycles containing:

  

• [[NREM Sleep]]

• [[REM Sleep]]

• [[Slow Wave Sleep]] (Deep sleep)

• [[Light Sleep]] (Stage 1–2)

  

Each cycle changes across the night — more deep sleep early, more REM later.

---

## Functional Role

  

Sleep Architecture regulates:

- Nervous system repair
    
- Memory consolidation
    
- Hormonal recovery
    
- Cortisol rhythm stability
    
- Emotional regulation
    
- Arousal recalibration
    

  

It directly interacts with:

- [[Arousal]]
    
- [[Nervous System Recalibration]]
    
- [[Cortisol Curve]]
    
- [[Dense Calm Potential]]
    

---

## Relationship to Circadian Rhythm

  

Sleep Architecture is shaped by the [[Circadian Rhythm]].

  

Circadian rhythms dictate:

• When sleep is initiated

• When REM dominance occurs

• When cortisol rises

• When melatonin drops

  

Architecture is the structure.

Circadian rhythm is the timing signal.

---

## Failure Modes

  

Sleep architecture degrades when:

- Circadian rhythm is disrupted
    
- Cortisol spikes at night
    
- Inconsistent sleep times
    
- Overtraining / underrecovery
    
- Blue light exposure late
    
- CNS overstimulation
    

  

This leads to:

Fragmented sleep.

Flat energy curve.

Poor arousal control.

---

## System Integration

  

Sleep Architecture supports:

- [[Instrumented Consciousness]] (through cognitive clarity)
    
- [[Power as the Agent of Transformation]] (through recovery)
    
- [[Momentum]] (through energy continuity)
    
- [[Central Axis]] stability
    

  

Disruption weakens:

- [[Directional Authority]]

---

## Practical Monitoring

  

Key indicators of healthy sleep architecture:

- Falling asleep within ~20 min
    
- Minimal night waking
    
- Feeling restored on waking
    
- Stable energy curve across day
    
- Low emotional volatility
