**Spine: Energy Flow & Mobility**

My spine is my main transmission line.
Everything runs through it — movement, signal, tension, release.

If my spine is stiff, my life becomes stiff.
If it’s fluid, everything else starts to follow.

I treat it less like bones and more like **current**.

**How I understand it:**

My spine is where:
	•	My nervous system travels
	•	My posture is organised
	•	My breath expands
	•	My energy distributes

When it’s compressed or locked, my whole system feels fragmented.
When it’s mobile and stacked, things circulate properly.

**What mobility actually means to me:**

Not just flexibility.
Not just strength.
But **smooth, controlled range from top to bottom.**

Neck → Thoracic → Lumbar → Sacrum

Not isolated.
Connected.

**My simple daily spine rules:**
	1.	I move my spine in all directions every day
Flexion. Extension. Rotation. Side-bending.
	2.	I treat stiffness as a signal, not a strength badge
If a section feels blocked, I don’t bully it — I explore it.
	3.	My posture is a living thing
Not “sit up straight”
Just: long spine, softened ribs, pelvis neutral.
	4.	My breath flows through my spine
When I inhale, I imagine air expanding up and down my spinal column.
When I exhale, tension drains out the same way.

**How this links to energy:**

When my spine moves freely:
	•	My breathing deepens
	•	My reactions slow down
	•	My fight-or-flight softens
	•	My focus sharpens
	•	My body speaks to my brain more clearly

Not spiritual woo.
Just **neuro-mechanics and flow**.

My spine isn’t just structure.
It’s the **highway between intention and action**.
