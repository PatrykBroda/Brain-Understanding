# Node: Friction Mapping Engine

  

Mechanism

Friction appears when internal signals, structural lines, or intentions misalign. It shows up as resistance, hesitation, or inefficiency. Instead of treating friction as failure, I read it as data revealing where the system needs adjustment.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel drag in movement, emotional resistance, breath catching, or hesitation before action. My structure or intention isn’t congruent with the direction I’m trying to move.

  

Instruction

When friction appears, pause half a beat, identify the misalignment, and realign structure, breath, or intention before continuing.
