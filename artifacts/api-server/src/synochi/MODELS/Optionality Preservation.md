Signal

I feel strongest when multiple paths remain open and none are prematurely collapsed.

  

Rule

Optionality is preserved through non-commitment, spacing, and refusal to over-identify with any single environment.

  

Protocol

- Avoid exclusive narratives (“this is my place”)
    
- Spread exposure across contexts
    
- Keep commitments light unless strategically chosen
    
- Reassess options regularly
    

  

Stop Condition

If I feel boxed in, claimed, or subtly obligated, optionality has been reduced.
