# Node: Hijacking Recognition

  

Mechanism

Hijacking occurs when a lower neural subsystem captures behavioural direction before conscious intent engages. It shows up as impulsive activation, tension spikes, or rapid shifts in arousal that override deliberate choice.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel a sudden narrowing of attention, quick breath changes, emotional surges, or instinctive movement patterns trying to take the lead.

  

Instruction

When I sense this takeover, pause one beat, inhale lightly through the nose, and redirect control back toward the central axis.
