
Capacity expands through identity-driven override of protective nervous system signals.

Mechanism  
Pain and resistance are reframed as evidence of growth.  
Emotional and physiological stop-signals are consciously pushed through.  
Identity ("I don’t quit") becomes the driver instead of current state comfort.

Strength  
Rapid capacity expansion.  
High tolerance for discomfort.  
Strong resilience under acute stress.

Cost / Risk  
Can suppress legitimate recovery signals.  
Increased risk of overtraining, burnout, and emotional suppression.  
Unsustainable as a constant operating mode.

Synochi Position  
A valid but high-cost pathway to expansion.  
Synochi prioritises regulation and progressive threshold training over chronic override.
