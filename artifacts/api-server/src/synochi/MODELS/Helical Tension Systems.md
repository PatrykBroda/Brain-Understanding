# Node: Helical Tension Systems

  

Mechanism

My body organises force through spirals, not straight lines. Helical tension distributes load across fascia, joints, and the axis, allowing power to travel smoothly while maintaining balance, adaptability, and control under pressure.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel rotational tension winding through my torso, hips, and limbs. Movements become more stable, elastic, and connected. When the helical system breaks, I feel linear collapse, stiffness, or imbalance.

  

Instruction

When force feels rigid or isolated, re-engage the spiral by rotating gently through the axis and letting tension wrap through the body instead of pushing straight.
