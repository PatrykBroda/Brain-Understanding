# Node: Distributed Awareness Stabilisation

  

Mechanism

Distributed awareness stabilises the system by spreading perception across multiple internal points instead of fixating on one. This prevents overwhelm, reduces threat bias, and keeps the nervous system operating in a wider, calmer field.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel perception soften and widen — awareness in back, ribs, hips, and breath at the same time. When distribution collapses, attention narrows, stress rises, and small signals feel bigger than they are.

  

Instruction

When awareness collapses into a single point, widen it deliberately — include more of the body in perception and stabilise the field.
