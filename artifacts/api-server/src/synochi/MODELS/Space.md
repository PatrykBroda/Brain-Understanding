
I experience space as the gap between stimulus and response.

Not a pause of hesitation — but a functional interval where choice becomes possible.

  

This is the zone where:

• reaction slows

• direction becomes selectable

• and my system reclaims authorship over its output

  

When my nervous system is dysregulated, this space collapses.

Stimulus → reaction happens instantly.

No sovereignty. No control.

  

When regulated, this space expands.

And inside it, I can:

• reorient

• contain energy

• redirect power

• choose response over reflex

  

This space is not passive.

It’s an active buffer field.

  

It’s where:

• hijacking is interrupted

• directionality is applied

• and perceptual filtering happens without strain

  

This is where my control actually lives.

  

Not in force.

Not in aggression.

  

But in the gap.
