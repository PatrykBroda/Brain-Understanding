Signal

Competitive energy can activate even when outcomes are neutral or positive.

  

Rule

Charge is information, not instruction; it does not require discharge through action.

  

Protocol

- Detect the rise in arousal
    
- Stay behaviourally minimal
    
- Allow the charge to decay without expression
    
- Return attention to task or body
    

  

Stop Condition

If I seek another round, comment, or comparison to settle the feeling, charge is directing behaviour.
