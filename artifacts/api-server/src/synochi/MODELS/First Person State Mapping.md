Definition

This note is where I map my lived internal states, not my theories.

It tracks how my nervous system configurations actually feel, shift, and reorganise from inside.

  

This is not conceptual.

It’s observational and embodied.

---

## What This Note Is For

  

I use this node to record:

  

• how regulation feels vs dysregulation

• how containment feels vs leakage

• how arousal presents in my body

• how my perception shifts under different loads

• how my nervous system transitions between states

  

This is about experience, not explanation.

---

## Example State Entries

  

I can log states like:

  

State: High Containment, Low Noise

– Body feels dense, heavy, stable

– Breath low and slow

– Jaw neutral

– Perception clear and narrow

– No urge to act

– Strong vertical centre

  

State: Aftershock Lightness

– Body feels hollow or light

– Slight derealisation

– Breath shallow but calm

– Peripheral vision increased

– Energy inconsistent

– Awareness wide but unfocused

  

Over time, patterns emerge.
