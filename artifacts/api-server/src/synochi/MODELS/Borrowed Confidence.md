## [[Borrowed Confidence]]

  

Type: [[Failure Mode Node]]

  

Definition:

A state where confidence is derived from [[System Optimisation]], [[External Validation]], or [[Assisted Success]] rather than [[Error Exposure]] and [[Authorship]].

  

Core Statement:

Confidence that has not survived error is not owned.

  

Mechanism:

Borrowed confidence forms when systems absorb friction, mask mistakes, or deliver correct outcomes before judgment is trained.

  

Primary Sources:

- [[Error Masking]]
    
- [[Over-Automation]]
    
- [[Predictive Assistance]]
    
- [[Premature Scaffolding]]
    
- [[Outcome Without Explanation]]
    

  

Symptoms:

- Certainty without causal understanding
    
- Inability to explain success
    
- Surprise or collapse under novel pressure
    
- Avoidance of friction or testing
    
- Reliance on system smoothness
    

  

Key Distinction:

- [[Earned Confidence]] — survives error, adapts under load
    
- Borrowed Confidence — evaporates on contact with reality
    

  

Downstream Risks:

- [[Brittle Competence]]
    
- [[False Mastery]]
    
- [[Agency Erosion]]
    
- [[Catastrophic Surprise]]
    

  

Corrective Responses:

- Restore [[Error Exposure]]
    
- Increase [[Resistance]]
    
- Remove [[Outcome Smoothing]]
    
- Force [[Post-Action Review]]
    
- Re-anchor effort in [[Authorship]]
    

  

Invariant:

Confidence must be paid for in friction to be durable.
