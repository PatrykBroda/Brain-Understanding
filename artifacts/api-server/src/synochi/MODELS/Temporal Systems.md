## Definition

  

Temporal Systems are the biological and neurological mechanisms that organise time inside the body.

  

Not just clocks.

They are internal timing architectures governing rhythm, recovery, energy, and state transitions.

---

## Core Functions

  

Temporal systems regulate:

  

• Sleep–wake cycling

• Hormonal release timing

• Energy availability

• Recovery rhythms

• Stress / adaptation windows

• Arousal baselines

  

They are the time-body interface.

---

Temporal Systems operate through three primary axes:

3. [[Cortisol Rhythm]]
    

  

These form the temporal spine of your system.
