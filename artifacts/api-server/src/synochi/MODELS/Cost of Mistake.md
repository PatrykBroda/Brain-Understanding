## [[Cost of Mistake]]

  

Type: [[Learning Integrity Node]]

  

Definition:

The degree to which an error produces felt consequence sufficient to train [[Judgment]], [[Self-Regulation]], and [[Authorship]].

  

Core Statement:

If mistakes are not felt, they cannot teach.

  

Mechanism:

Learning requires that errors carry:

- perceptible impact
    
- responsibility ownership
    
- recovery effort
    

  

When systems absorb or soften this cost, [[Error Masking]] and [[Borrowed Confidence]] emerge.

  

Dimensions of Cost:

- [[Time Cost]] — delays, lost momentum
    
- [[Effort Cost]] — rework, correction, fatigue
    
- [[Cognitive Cost]] — confusion, uncertainty
    
- [[Reputational Cost]] — accountability, trust
    
- [[Opportunity Cost]] — missed alternatives
    

  

Cost Suppression Sources:

- [[Over-Automation]]
    
- [[Assisted Success]]
    
- [[Predictive Correction]]
    
- [[Outcome Smoothing]]
    
- [[Intent Inference]]
    

  

Healthy Cost Profile:

- Errors are survivable
    
- Recovery is possible
    
- Feedback is immediate
    
- Responsibility is clear
    

  

System Safeguards:

- Preserve [[Error Exposure]]
    
- Avoid premature optimisation
    
- Require [[Post-Action Review]]
    
- Delay assistance during execution
    
- Tie correction to [[Authorship]]
    

  

Invariant:

Learning scales with the cost of mistake, not the frequency of success.

  

Failure Modes if Absent:

- [[False Mastery]]
    
- [[Skill Atrophy]]
    
- [[Risk Blindness]]
    
- [[Catastrophic Surprise]]
