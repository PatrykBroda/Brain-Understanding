Signal

Playful banter can coexist with residual competitive charge; tone may be relaxed while arousal briefly persists.

  

Rule

Banter often functions as social lubrication, but light verbal or physical digs mark the final discharge of competitive tension.

  

Protocol

- Allow light expression without escalation
    
- Note internal charge without acting on it
    
- Do not justify, replay, or narrate afterward
    
- Let the interaction close naturally
    

  

Stop Condition

If banter loops into replay, self-analysis, or urge to re-engage, the charge has not fully dissipated.
