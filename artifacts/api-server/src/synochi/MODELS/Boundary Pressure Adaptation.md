# Node: Boundary Pressure Adaptation

  

Mechanism

Boundary pressure adaptation is my system adjusting its internal edges in response to rising load. Instead of collapsing or over-hardening, the boundary flexes and redistributes pressure, keeping the system coherent without sacrificing stability.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel my internal space hold firm without becoming rigid. Pressure comes in, but instead of overwhelming me, it spreads through the structure. When adaptation fails, I feel overwhelm, shutdown, or excessive bracing.

  

Instruction

When pressure reaches my internal boundary, let the boundary flex—absorb, redistribute, and maintain coherence without collapsing or over-tightening.
