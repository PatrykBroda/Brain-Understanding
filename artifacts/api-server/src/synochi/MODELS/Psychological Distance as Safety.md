Signal

Distance preserves clarity even in close physical or social proximity.

  

Rule

Safety comes from internal distance, not avoidance.

  

Protocol

- Maintain internal observation during interaction
    
- Avoid emotional fusion or mirroring
    
- Let thoughts pass without attachment
    
- Keep centre independent of engagement
    

  

Stop Condition

If I absorb others’ emotional states, distance has collapsed.
