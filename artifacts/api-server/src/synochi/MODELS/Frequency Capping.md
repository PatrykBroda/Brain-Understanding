## [[Frequency Capping]]

  

Type: [[Exposure Control Node]]

  

Definition:

A constraint that limits how often an input, idea, prompt, or intervention is allowed to appear in order to prevent [[Runaway Replication]] and preserve [[Self-Regulation]].

  

Core Statement:

What appears too often becomes dominant, regardless of value.

  

Mechanism:

Frequency capping interrupts [[Replication Rate]] by:

- reducing repeated exposure
    
- preventing saturation of [[Attention]]
    
- stopping escalation through familiarity
    

  

This preserves the system’s ability to evaluate rather than absorb.

  

What Gets Capped:

- [[Prompts]]
    
- [[Suggestions]]
    
- [[Interventions]]
    
- [[Notifications]]
    
- [[Corrective Feedback]]
    

  

Why It Matters:

Unchecked frequency causes:

- [[Thought Parasitism]]
    
- [[False Urgency]]
    
- [[Obedience Without Wisdom]]
    
- [[Desensitisation]]
    
- [[Agency Erosion]]
    

  

System Use (Synochi):

- Limit repeated guidance during [[Execution]]
    
- Reduce corrective prompts after initial exposure
    
- Enforce silence once orientation is achieved
    
- Space resistance to maintain impact
    

  

Adaptive Scaling:

Frequency caps tighten as:

- [[Signal Quality]] improves
    
- [[User Capability]] increases
    
- [[Borrowed Confidence]] is detected
    

  

Invariant:

Impact decays with repetition; authority requires scarcity.

  

Failure Modes if Absent:

- [[Noise Accumulation]]
    
- [[Engagement Without Growth]]
    
- [[Context Collapse]]
    
- [[Comfort Dependency]]
