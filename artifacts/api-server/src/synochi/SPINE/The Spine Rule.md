
# The Spine Rule

  

The spine is the central processing trunk of the system.

All uncertain, ambiguous, or undefined signals should be routed back to the spine.

  

### Why?

  

Because the spine is:

  

• Your primary structural axis

• Your force conductor

• Your state regulator

• Your geometry reference

• Your movement map

• Your safety detector

• Your control centre for arousal & directionality

  

Meaning:

  

If a new concept, behaviour, input, or node doesn’t have a home yet?

  

It belongs to the spine until proven otherwise.
