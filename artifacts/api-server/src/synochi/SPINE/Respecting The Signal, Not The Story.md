Signal

My body registers truth before my mind constructs explanation.

  

Rule

Stories are optional; signals are authoritative.

  

Protocol

- Trust the initial bodily read
    
- Do not immediately narrate or justify
    
- Act on signal through boundary or constraint
    
- Allow meaning to emerge later, if at all
    

  

Stop Condition

If I build a story to legitimise a feeling, the signal has already been diluted.
