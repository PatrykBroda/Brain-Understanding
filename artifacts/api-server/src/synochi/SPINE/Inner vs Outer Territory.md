## Definition

  

Territory is the domain over which authority, perception and power operate.

  

Not ownership.

Not control.

Jurisdiction.

---

## Two Domains of Territory

  

Territory splits into:

  

• Inner Territory

• Outer Territory

  

They function differently, obey different rules, and require different forms of authority.

  

Most people confuse them.

That’s why they lose both.

---

## Inner Territory

  

Inner Territory is your internal domain:

Your body, mind, nervous system, perception field, energy system.

  

It includes:

- Sensation
    
- Thought
    
- Emotion
    
- Arousal state
    
- Attention
    
- Directional authority
    
- Somatic field

### Inner Territory Laws

  

Inner territory operates on:

• Awareness

• Regulation

• Perception sovereignty

• Directional authority

  

You don’t dominate it.

You inhabit and govern it.

  

Failure of inner territory control results in:

- Hijacking
    
- Fragmentation
    
- Identity diffusion
    
- Emotional possession
    
- Directional collapse

Outer Territory is your external domain:

Physical space, social environment, time, movement, interaction.

  

It includes:

- Physical space
    
- social fields
    
- work domains
    
- competitive contexts
    
- environments you enter
    
- fields of influence

Outer territory operates on:

- Boundary collapse
