## Constraint-Driven Reality

  

Type: Governing Reality Model

Status: Canonical (Locked)

Function: Establishes constraints as the primary truth filter for all decisions, actions, and interpretations.

---

### Definition

  

Constraint-Driven Reality asserts that reality is revealed through constraints, not possibilities.

  

What survives constraint is real.

What requires exemption, justification, or special conditions is fantasy.

  

This node is the ontological backbone of the entire system.

All other nodes operate inside it.

---

### Core Doctrine

- Constraints are truth.
    
- Reality does not negotiate.
    
- What cannot survive pressure is not real.
    
- Elegance is secondary to survivability.
    

---

### Why This Node Exists

  

Most systems fail because they privilege:

- ideals over limits,
    
- intelligence over capacity,
    
- intention over load,
    
- narrative over physics.
    

  

This creates plans that sound good but collapse under:

- time pressure,
    
- fatigue,
    
- uncertainty,
    
- responsibility,
    
- social friction.
    

  

This node exists to ensure that only what survives reality is allowed to guide action.

### Reality Filtering Rule

  

Any idea, plan, or desire must answer:

  

“What constraint kills this?”

  

If the answer is unclear, the idea is under-specified.

If the answer is ignored, the idea is fantasy.

---

1. Survive constraints

3. Then optimise
