Signal

I can perceive hierarchy clearly without needing to climb, defend, or correct it.

  

Rule

Hierarchy is contextual and temporary; attachment to position introduces unnecessary tension.

  

Protocol

- Acknowledge hierarchy without performing within it
    
- Do not seek affirmation or correction
    
- Operate to my own standards regardless of rank
    
- Let outcomes, not positioning, speak
    

  

Stop Condition

If I feel pulled to prove, resist, or redefine hierarchy, attachment has formed.
