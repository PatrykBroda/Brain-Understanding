### Definition

  

Directionality is the principle that power is only meaningful when it has a vector.

Energy without direction becomes noise.

Focus without direction becomes drift.

  

Direction is how force, intention, and attention are channelled through my axis.

- Axis over hijacking

- Direction as control, not speed
