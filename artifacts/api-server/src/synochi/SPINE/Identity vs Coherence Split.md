## Identity vs Coherence Split

  

Type: Core Spine Node

Status: Canonical (Week-One Architecture)

Function: Prevents identity maintenance from overriding system coherence.

---

### Definition

  

Identity vs Coherence Split describes the failure mode where maintaining a self-image takes priority over maintaining internal and external coherence.

  

Identity asks: “Who am I being?”

Coherence asks: “Is the system stable and aligned?”

  

When identity leads, coherence decays.

When coherence leads, identity becomes irrelevant.

---

### Core Doctrine

- Identity is descriptive, not directive.
    
- Coherence precedes character.
    
- Most self-sabotage is identity defence.
    
- Stability outperforms self-expression.
    

---

### Why This Node Exists

  

Identity is sticky. It:

- resists correction,
    
- seeks confirmation,
    
- interprets feedback as threat,
    
- prefers consistency over truth.
    

  

Under pressure, identity-based systems:

- double down instead of adjusting,
    
- escalate instead of pausing,
    
- justify instead of correcting.
    

  

This node exists to ensure governance beats self-image.

### Coherence Priority Stack

  

This node enforces the following order:

1. Physiological coherence
    
2. Perceptual accuracy
    
3. Constraint alignment
    
4. Cybernetic correction
    
5. Outcome stability
    
6. Identity (if needed at all)
    

  

Identity placed higher than #6 corrupts all layers below it.

---

### Identity as Lagging Indicator

  

In this system:

- identity is a trail,
    
- coherence is the engine.
    

  

You don’t act because “that’s who you are.”

You become who you are because coherence was maintained repeatedly.

---

### Correction Protocol

  

When identity interference is detected:

1. Remove narrative (“I am / I should”)
    
2. Name the state (“coherent / fragmented”)
    
3. Apply smallest correction
    
4. Delay explanation
    
5. Resume only if coherence returns
