# Behaviour Is The Truth Carrier

## Principle
Observable behaviour provides evidence that praise, agreement, affirmation or stated intention alone cannot establish.

What a person does after receiving information is a stronger indicator of its impact than positive sentiment about it. Repeated action also provides evidence for changes in self-concept.

## Evidence Boundary
Behaviour is evidence, not automatic certainty about motive, character or identity. Interpret interactions cautiously; repeated or outcome-supported signals carry more weight than a single interaction.
