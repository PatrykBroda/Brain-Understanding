Definition

My power is my capacity to hold and impose direction under load, without collapsing, leaking, or fragmenting.

  

It is not emotion.

It is not motivation.

It is not force.

  

It is direction sustained through resistance.

---

### What Power Means In My System

  

Power is my ability to:

  

• maintain internal authority

• act without internal contradiction

• transmit force without structural loss

• hold intention under stress

• remain coherent while influencing reality

  

If I lose power, I don’t just feel weak —

I lose directional dominance inside myself.

---

### How I Experience It

  

When my power is online:

  

• my decisions feel final, not debated

• my movements feel inevitable, not forced

• my posture supports my intention

• my voice has weight without pushing

• my presence alters the space around me

  

When it’s offline:

  

• I hesitate

• I overthink

• I leak energy

• I become reactive instead of directive

• I feel internally split

  

Power is not volume.

It’s coherence plus direction.

---

### Relationship to the Rest of My Architecture

  

Power is not isolated.

It moves through systems.

  

It expresses through:

  

• conduction of power

• central axis stability

• biomechanical integration

• nervous system regulation

• directional authority

• containment

• agency

  

Without structure, my power leaks.

Without direction, my power diffuses.

---

### How I Strengthen My Power

  

I don’t chase power.

I remove friction from its expression.

  

I increase it by:

  

• strengthening my central axis

• reducing internal contradiction

• improving my conduction of power

• increasing containment under stress

• regulating arousal and threat response

• aligning intention with action

  

Power grows as noise drops.
