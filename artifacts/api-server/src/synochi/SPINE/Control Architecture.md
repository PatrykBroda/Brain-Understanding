
### Definition

  

Control Architecture is how authority is managed inside the system.

It governs how signals are routed, dampened, amplified, blocked, or transferred under pressure.

  

This is not psychology.

It is systems engineering applied to self.

---

### Core Concepts

- Dials, gates, dampers, limiters, buffers, couplings
    
- Pivots vs switches
    
- Central Reorientation vs Hijacking
    
- Directional authority transfer
    
- Voluntary vs automatic system control
    
- System collision / co-regulation
    
- Control handover logic
    
- State gating & flow management
    

---

### Models & Metaphors

- Control Panel Model
    
    Not one switch — multiple control surfaces
    
    Each with different ranges and effects
    
- Hydraulic System Model
    
    Pressure routed through channels
    
    Not fought against
    
- Authority Transfer Model
    
    Control shifts between systems
    
    With or without consent
    

---

### BJJ & Movement Applications

- Managing decision layers under pressure
    
- Maintaining strategic control during energy spikes
    
- Preventing reactive hijack in scrambles
    
- Controlling tempo without forcing pace
    
- Decoupling emotional spike from motor output
    
- Choosing when to engage automatic response vs override it
    

---

### Synochi Functions

  

Control Architecture enables:

- Directionality enforcement
    
- Hijack resistance
    
- State stability under stress
    
- System coherence during collisions
    
- Functional use of perturbation
    

  

Without it:

→ Systems get hijacked

→ Reflex overrides intention

→ Power becomes unmanaged
