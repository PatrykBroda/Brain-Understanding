Signal

Energy remains stable when limits are explicit.

  

Rule

Unbounded systems leak energy through decision fatigue and overexposure.

  

Protocol

- Define clear limits on time, access, and effort
    
- Default to fewer commitments
    
- Say no through structure, not explanation
    
- Let constraints do the filtering
    

  

Stop Condition

If energy drains without obvious cause, a constraint is missing.
