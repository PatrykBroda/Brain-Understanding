  

Signal

Consistency of behaviour stabilises identity more reliably than internal self-definition.

  

Rule

Identity emerges from repeated actions under constraint, not from self-description.

  

Protocol

- Act to the same standard across environments
    
- Avoid adjusting behaviour to suit audience
    
- Let repetition do the signalling
    
- Trust time to reveal alignment or mismatch
    

  

Stop Condition

If I change tone, effort, or boundaries to manage perception, the anchor has slipped.
