Definition

My directional authority is my ability to choose and hold a direction of action, attention, or force without being overridden by internal noise or external interference.

  

It is not dominance over others.

It is dominance over my internal vector.

---

### What Directional Authority Means In My System

  

Directional authority is the line between:

  

• reaction and response

• drift and direction

• impulse and decision

• fragmentation and coherence

  

When I have directional authority,

my system follows one vector, not ten competing ones.

  

When I lose it,

my energy splinters.

---

### How I Experience It

  

When my directional authority is strong:

  

• my actions feel simple, not conflicted

• my attention stays forward

• my body aligns behind my decisions

• distractions lose power

• I don’t negotiate with noise

  

When it’s weak:

  

• I hesitate

• I second-guess

• I get pulled by multiple impulses

• my actions feel scattered

• my energy disperses

  

Directional authority is the difference between being moved and moving.

---

### Relationship To Power

  

Power is the capacity.

Directional authority is the steering.

  

Without power, authority collapses under load.

Without authority, power goes everywhere and nowhere.

  

They are inseparable in my system.

---

### Relationship To My Architecture

  

Directional authority links directly with:

  

• central axis stability

• power conduction

• system agency

• response vs reaction

• arousal regulation

• fragmentation detection

• central reorientation

  

It’s the command signal that prevents hijacking.

---

### How I Strengthen It

  

I strengthen my directional authority by:

  

• reducing internal contradiction

• grounding decisions in my central axis

• naming my direction before acting

• resisting impulse hijacking

• maintaining arousal containment

• training response over reaction

  

Authority grows as internal debate decreases.
