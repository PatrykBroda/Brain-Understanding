# Centreline Authority

## Definition
Centreline Authority is the capacity to hold your internal line under pressure — so external chaos doesn’t hijack decision-making.

It is the *embodied version* of: standards, pacing, restraint.

---

## Core Law
**If the centre moves, everything moves.**
