## Definition

  

Spiral Structural Intelligence is the body’s capacity to store force elastically through helical tension and return it through structural recoil rather than linear collapse.

  

It is not strength.

It is geometry conducting power.

---

## Core Principle

  

> Power is not created by force.

> It is stored through structure and released through spiral geometry.

  

Linear systems burn energy.

Spiral systems recycle it.

## Fundamental Components

  

### 1. Spiral Energy Storage

  

The body stores force through spiral tension in fascia and joints.

This allows impact to be absorbed and held rather than collapsing into joints.

  

When functional:

• Force coils

• Energy distributes

• Structure remains elastic

  

When dysfunctional:

• Collapse occurs

• Energy leaks

### 2. Force Return (Structural Recoil)

  

Stored force is released as movement, redirection, or acceleration.

  

This recoil is:

• Elastic

• Directional

• Efficient

• Low-leakage

  

It’s the difference between muscle strain and structural spring.
