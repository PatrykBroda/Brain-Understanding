Signal

Correct timing often matters more than correct action.

  

Rule

Action taken too early or too late degrades outcome regardless of quality.

  

Protocol

- Delay action until pressure clarifies necessity
    
- Observe rhythm of environment before moving
    
- Act decisively once timing is clear
    
- Stop immediately after execution
    

  

Stop Condition

If I act to relieve tension rather than necessity, timing has been violated.
