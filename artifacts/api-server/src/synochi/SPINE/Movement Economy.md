**Node: Movement Economy (Updated)**

  

**Mechanism**

Movement economy emerges when every action follows the cleanest path of force with no excess tension, overcorrection, or emotional charge. The system conserves energy by aligning structure, breath, and intention into one coherent line.

  

**Behavioural Expression**

I feel smoothness, reduced effort, minimal noise, and precise transitions. When economy drops, I notice gripping, overreaching, or unnecessary micro-movements.

  

**Instruction**

When movement feels wasteful or noisy, strip away tension, return to the axis, and execute only what the moment actually requires.
