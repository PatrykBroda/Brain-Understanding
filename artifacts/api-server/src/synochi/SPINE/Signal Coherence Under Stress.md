# Node: Signal Coherence Under Stress

  

Mechanism

Signal coherence under stress is my system keeping breath, tension, intention, and perception aligned when external pressure rises. Instead of letting signals scatter or compete, they stay synchronised as one unified channel.

  

Behavioural Expression

I feel coordinated breath, steady tension lines, and clear perception even when stress climbs. When coherence breaks, signals desync — breath shortens, tension spikes, and attention fractures.

  

Instruction

When stress increases, unify the signals by slowing the exhale, restacking the axis, and aligning breath with intention before acting.
