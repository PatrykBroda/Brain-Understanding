# Cricket Intelligence Hub

## Purpose
The Cricket Intelligence Hub is the central navigation and integration layer for Synochi’s cricket-specific knowledge.

It connects the universal mechanisms, role-specific intelligence, tactical models, diagnostic patterns, drill structures, athlete models, and historical context required to understand cricket performance as a system.

## Scope
The hub should organise cricket intelligence across:
- batting
- bowling
- fielding
- match intelligence
- perception
- decision-making
- pressure
- risk
- tactical adaptation
- athlete-specific tendencies
- diagnostics
- training interventions

## Function
The hub allows Synochi to move between:
- what happened
- why it happened
- what mechanism likely produced it
- whether the pattern is stable or situational
- what should be trained, tested, or adapted next
