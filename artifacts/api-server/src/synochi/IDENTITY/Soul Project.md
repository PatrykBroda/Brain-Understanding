### Co-Regulation with Technology

  

I’m not trying to use technology to control my nervous system.

I’m building a way to co-regulate with it.

  

Most biofeedback tools just give data – heart rate, HRV, stress scores, brainwaves.

But without a structure to interpret those signals, people either get confused, dependent, or disconnected from their own body.

  

My model changes that.

  

It acts as a translation layer between raw physiological data and embodied understanding.

  

Instead of just seeing numbers, I can see:

• where I am in my arousal curve

• whether I’m approaching fragmentation

• if my stress tolerance window is expanding or contracting

• where pressure leaks or stabilises

  

Technology becomes a mirror, not a controller.

  

I use it to:

• recognise my internal state

• apply the right intervention (breath, posture, attention, movement)

• and slowly train my system to self-correct without relying on the device.

  

The goal isn’t dependence.

The goal is internal instrumentation and sovereignty.

  

Technology supports the process.

My nervous system still leads it.
