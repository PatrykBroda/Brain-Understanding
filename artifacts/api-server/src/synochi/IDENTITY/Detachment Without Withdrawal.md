Signal
I can remain detached without disengaging or disappearing.

Rule
Detachment preserves clarity; withdrawal collapses optionality.

Protocol
	•	Stay present without emotional investment
	•	Participate functionally, not socially
	•	Avoid signalling exit or disdain
	•	Maintain calm availability without attachment

Stop Condition
If detachment turns into avoidance or cynicism, withdrawal has begun.
