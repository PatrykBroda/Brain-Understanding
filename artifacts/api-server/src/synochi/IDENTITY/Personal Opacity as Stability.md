Signal
Withholding personal information preserves internal stability and prevents unnecessary entanglement.

Rule
Not all environments earn access to my personal narrative; opacity is a form of self-regulation, not avoidance.

Protocol
	•	Share only what is functionally required
	•	Keep answers short and non-expansive
	•	Avoid volunteering personal context
	•	Redirect naturally to task or training

Stop Condition
If I feel exposed, drained, or subtly evaluated after sharing, opacity was breached.
