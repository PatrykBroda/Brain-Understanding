# Identity Stabilises Around Long-Term Direction

## Definition
Identity coherence grows through sustained effort toward goals aligned with personal values and long-term direction. Meaningful pursuit reinforces a stable sense of who one is becoming.

## Boundary
Long-term direction informs identity through repeated behaviour, commitments and values. It does not require every temporary state, role or outcome to become part of self-definition.
