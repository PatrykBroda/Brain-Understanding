Signal

I can show respect without yielding authority or diminishing my internal centre.

  

Rule

Respect is behavioural and situational; submission is internal and optional.

  

Protocol

- Acknowledge others cleanly
    
- Follow local rules without self-reduction
    
- Do not seek approval or exemption
    
- Maintain internal standards regardless of role
    

  

Stop Condition

If respect turns into deference, silence, or self-editing, centre has shifted outward.
