Signal

Someone owning a space does not automatically grant them internal authority over me.

  

Rule

Authority is embodied through behaviour and coherence, not titles, roles, or property.

  

Protocol

- Treat ownership as logistical, not hierarchical
    
- Respect rules without internal submission
    
- Assess authority through conduct, not position
    
- Keep internal centre independent of external roles
    

  

Stop Condition

If I start deferring internally because of someone’s title or ownership, authority has been misattributed.
