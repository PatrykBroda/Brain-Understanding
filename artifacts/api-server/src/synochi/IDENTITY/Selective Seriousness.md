Signal

Not everything deserves full seriousness.

  

Rule

Over-seriousness inflates stakes and drains clarity.

  

Protocol

- Reserve seriousness for irreversible or high-impact decisions
    
- Treat minor events lightly and briefly
    
- Avoid theatrical concern
    
- Keep tone proportionate to consequence
    

  

Stop Condition

If everything feels heavy or urgent, seriousness has lost selectivity.
