Signal

I can detect insecurity in leadership when control, validation, or status is subtly asserted rather than embodied.

  

Rule

When an owner or authority figure leads with probing, comparison, or performative behaviour, their centre is externally referenced.

  

Protocol

- Remain respectful and neutral
    
- Do not mirror insecurity or challenge authority
    
- Reduce personal exposure
    
- Interact strictly on functional terms
    

  

Stop Condition

If I feel the urge to correct, dominate, or disengage emotionally, the insecurity has already been identified and distance is required.
