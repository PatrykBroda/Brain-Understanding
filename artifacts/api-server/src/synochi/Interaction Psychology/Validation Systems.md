When I say validation systems, I don’t mean social approval in a surface way.

I mean the primitive circuits inside me that decide whether I am safe, seen, dominant, accepted, or excluded.

  

These systems evolved long before language.

They don’t care about logic — they care about position, belonging, and survival relevance.

  

When my validation system is dominated by primitive layers:

- I seek approval to reduce threat.
    
- I chase signals of dominance to avoid marginalisation.
    
- I react to rejection as if it’s physical danger.
    

  

When it’s integrated:

- I don’t need constant external confirmation.
    
- I can feel social signals without being hijacked by them.
    
- My identity becomes internally anchored, not socially rented.
    

  

I can feel validation happening like a signal loop:

External input → Primitive interpretation → Body response → Identity reinforcement.

  

If I don’t interrupt this loop,

my nervous system starts living for feedback instead of truth.

  

Real power for me is when validation shifts from:

external reward → to → internal coherence

  

That’s where my centre of gravity moves back inside.
