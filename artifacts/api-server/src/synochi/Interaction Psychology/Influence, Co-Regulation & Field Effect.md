**Influence, Co-Regulation & Field Effect**

My nervous system does not operate in isolation.
It exists within a field of other nervous systems and constantly interacts with them.

Humans are not only self-regulating — we are **co-regulating organisms**.

Children demonstrate this most clearly.
They instinctively move towards the most regulated, stable and safe nervous system in any environment.
Not the loudest.
Not the most dominant.
The most secure.

This isn’t learned — it’s biological.

A regulated nervous system creates a **gravity field of safety**.
It communicates stability without words, through posture, tone, micro-movement, breath and presence.

An unregulated nervous system creates tension, unpredictability and noise.
Others feel this even if no one speaks.

This is why:
	•	Children gravitate toward certain people in a room
	•	Animals behave differently around certain humans
	•	Teams perform better around certain leaders
	•	Some people calm a space simply by entering it

A regulated system lowers the threat level of the environment for others.
An unregulated system increases it.

This is not metaphorical — it’s neurobiological.

When my nervous system is coherent, stable, and grounded:
	•	I become a reference point for others
	•	People unconsciously align to that regulation
	•	The environment itself becomes less reactive

Influence, therefore, is not primarily spoken.
It is transmitted through **state**.

Leadership is regulation before language.
Presence before instruction.
Stability before direction.

My responsibility is not just to regulate for myself,
but to understand that my state conditions the field around me.
