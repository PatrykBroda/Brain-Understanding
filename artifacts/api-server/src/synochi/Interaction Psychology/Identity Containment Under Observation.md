Signal

When I am being observed, filmed, or subtly assessed, my system naturally tightens identity boundaries.

  

Rule

Observation environments amplify projection; containment preserves coherence and prevents narrative hijack.

  

Protocol

- Reduce expressive range
    
- Default to fundamentals and basics
    
- Avoid storytelling or self-description
    
- Let behaviour remain minimal and legible
    

  

Stop Condition

If I feel performative, self-conscious, or tempted to “show range,” containment has loosened.
