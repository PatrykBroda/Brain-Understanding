Prompt for Replit
FRAME – Camp Mode & Calendar Integration

Replace the generic planner with a Camp system.

Core principle:
FRAME is not a productivity app. The planner exists to support fight preparation, not task management.

Features:

1. Camp Creation
- Fight date
- Opponent (optional)
- Promotion (optional)
- Weight class
- Number of rounds
- Location (optional)
- Notes

2. Countdown
Display days remaining until competition throughout the app.

3. Camp Phases
Automatically determine:
- Build
- Develop
- Sharpen
- Fight Week

Adapt coaching and reminders based on the current phase.

4. Google Calendar Integration
Allow users to securely connect Google Calendar.

Import:
- Training sessions
- Existing events
- Rest days
- Travel

FRAME must never modify the user's calendar without explicit confirmation.

Users can optionally add FRAME sessions to Google Calendar.

5. Training Planner
Allow scheduling:
- Sparring
- Wrestling
- BJJ
- Striking
- Conditioning
- Recovery
- Mobility

Each session includes:
- Date
- Time
- Duration
- Coach
- Objective

6. Context-Aware Coaching
When Camp Mode is active, all AI responses should consider:
- Days until fight
- Current camp phase
- Recent training load
- Recovery
- Competition proximity

7. Future Expansion
Design the architecture so Camp Mode can later support:
- Opponent scouting
- Weight-cut tracking
- Video analysis linked to a specific camp
- Camp reports
- Fight review after competition
- Multiple historical camps

The planner should feel calm, premium, and performance-focused—not like a generic calendar or productivity tool.
One final idea

I wouldn't even call the tab Planner.

I'd call it Camp.

For an MMA athlete, "Camp" is immediately meaningful. It's emotionally tied to preparation, discipline, and competition. It also gives every feature—calendar, countdown, scheduled sessions, reminders, opponent notes, weight tracking, and AI coaching—a single unifying purpose instead of feeling like unrelated productivity tools. I think that naming change alone makes the app feel much more authentic to the sport.
