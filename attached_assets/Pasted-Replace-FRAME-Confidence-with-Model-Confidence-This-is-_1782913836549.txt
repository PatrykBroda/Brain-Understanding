3. Give the AI uncertainty

This is the feature almost nobody builds.

Instead of only showing what FRAME knows…

Show what it's questioning.

Example:

FRAME Hypotheses

• You appear to perform better with clear tactical objectives.

• You may become outcome-focused before competition.

• Confidence: Low

Notice the wording:

may

That makes the AI feel scientific instead of magical.

Later it can confirm or reject hypotheses.
