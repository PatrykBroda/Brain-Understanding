The feature that becomes your moat

If I could build one feature that no other MMA app has, it would be this:

Every uploaded video, every chat, every session recap, every competition reflection, and every weekly mission contributes to a single evolving Athlete Graph.

Not a memory.

Not notes.

A graph of evidence.

FRAME doesn't remember that you said you struggle with exits—it knows that because:

14 analysed videos show it.
Your coach mentioned it twice.
You admitted it after sparring.
It appeared again in competition footage.

When FRAME eventually says:

"This pattern has appeared in 19 separate observations across the last six months. It's now your highest-confidence technical weakness."

...that's no longer a chatbot talking. That's a performance system that has built an evidence-based understanding of the athlete over time.

That's the moat. Not the chat. Not the UI. The continuously evolving athlete intelligence model that everything in the app feeds into.
