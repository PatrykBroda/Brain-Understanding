One suggestion I'd make

I wouldn't lock Video Analysis completely.

Instead:

Free: 1 video analysis per month
FRAME+: Unlimited video analysis

That's because video analysis is likely to become your biggest conversion feature. If users never experience it, they don't know what they're paying for. One high-quality free analysis can be the feature that convinces them FRAME is worth £6.99/month. The same principle applies to the Athlete Model: let people see enough to appreciate its value, then make the more advanced, evolving insights part of FRAME+.
