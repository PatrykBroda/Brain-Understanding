2. User Ability Vocabulary Scaling

If a white belt gets:

proprioceptive retention mechanics

they're gone.

Need layered language.

Example:

Beginner:

Keep your elbows tight and protect space.

Advanced:

Focus on maintaining inside position and controlling the hip line.

Same lesson.

Different delivery.

3. Keyword Library

Massive opportunity.

Common mistakes

Not just dictionary definitions.

Think:

FRAME Knowledge Base

not glossary.

6. Competition Countdown Wording

Current:

7. Bullet Points > Paragraphs

Especially for combat athletes.

Instead of:

You should focus on guard retention because...

Use:

Today's Focus

• Guard retention
• Hip mobility
• Frame recovery

Why:

• Passed 4 times last session
• Recovery speed decreasing

Much easier to scan.

Higher-Level UX Fixes
9. Slideshow Once Every 24 Hours

Good.

Otherwise it becomes banner blindness.

Treat it like:

Daily Briefing

not a carousel.

10. Conversation Re-Entry Logic

Current issue:

User returns hours later.

AI acts like conversation never stopped.

Feels weird.

Suggested:

Under 2 Hours

Resume.

2-24 Hours

Show:

Welcome back.

Last focus:
Guard retention.

Anything changed since then?
Over 24 Hours

Fresh session.

Show:

Daily Check-In

How did training go?
10 Second Countdown

I wouldn't do a visible countdown.

Feels artificial.

Instead:

Welcome back.

Still working on this?

If ignored:

Quick check-in:
Any training completed today?

Much cleaner.

Potentially powerful.

Not punishment.

Behavior shaping.

Phone vibration could support this.

But keep it rare.

Otherwise users disable notifications.
