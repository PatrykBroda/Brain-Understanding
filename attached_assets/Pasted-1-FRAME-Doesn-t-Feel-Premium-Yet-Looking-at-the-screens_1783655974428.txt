1. FRAME+ Doesn't Feel Premium Yet

Looking at the screenshot, the problem isn't the design.

It's the hierarchy.

Right now it feels like a navigation card.

It should feel like entering another tier of the operating system.

Currently I see:

FRAME+

Unlimited coaching • full history • full mission • £6.99/month →

That doesn't create desire.

I'd redesign it like this
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FRAME+

Your Athlete Model never stops evolving.

Unlimited coaching

Unlimited video analysis

Continuous calibration

£6.99 / month

[ Unlock FRAME+ ]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Much less text.

Much more confidence.

Even better...

Give it movement.

Not flashy.

Imagine the border has an almost imperceptible pulse every 8–10 seconds.

The orb already breathes.

FRAME+ should too.

Almost like:

"This system is alive."

Don't Sell Features

Sell Identity.

Bad:

Unlimited Coaching

Good:

Train with a model that evolves with you.

Bad:

Unlimited Messages

Good:

No limits on calibration.

Bad:

Premium

Good:

Continuous Athlete Model

You're selling the relationship.

Not the messages.

2. Video Analysis

This is where I think FRAME could become something nobody else has.

Most AI video apps do this:

Upload

↓

AI analyses

↓

Big paragraph

↓

Done

That's boring.

FRAME should feel like an assistant coach reviewing film.

The FRAME Analysis Flow
Step 1

Upload

or

Paste YouTube link

Step 2

FRAME asks

What are we looking for today?

Not:

"What sport?"

Instead

Overall

Striking

Footwork

Distance

Or...

User types:

"Why do I keep getting caught exiting?"

Step 3

FRAME watches.

Don't immediately produce coaching.

Instead...

Building movement model...

Finding exchanges...

Tracking movement...

Comparing recurring behaviours...

Building observations...

This feels premium.

Not a report.

A review.
